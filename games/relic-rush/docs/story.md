# Relic Rush: A Light Below

**Status:** One hundred expeditions in five chapters of twenty, one per epoch of four restorations. Every expedition has a scene on its first clear; the ten story restorations have a scene when they are placed. The reply choice is at the Expedition camp (16).

**Play fantasy:** Bring a working harbour back to life with a present-day expedition crew and find the keeper signalling from under the cliff. The feeling is curious, warm-hearted pulp adventure. Each repair gives the crew something they can use on the next leg of the search.

## What is broken

The camp is abandoned, the old well is fouled, and the quay wall is failing. The broken electric relay leaves the lower quay without its regular warning. Ines warns boats by hand across a flooded gap. The crew needs water, timber and access to the harbour register and the keeper's archive.

## Truth timeline

1. The original harbor builders built the lower quay and fitted a hand shutter to warn boats away from the reef. Generations later, Tomas and his sister Ines fitted an electric relay. A lamp at the old lookout can answer someone at the warning shutter.
2. Tomas and Ines worked together. When the quay wall cracked, Tomas closed the room; Ines left to fetch repair plans and supplies. Tomas promised to maintain the warning, but a storm cut the cable. He tried to splice it and left his pliers at the break. He believes Ines did not return.
3. Recently Ines returned by a different shore. She found the room flooded and, unable to reach camp, used its hand shutter to send the old warning. She changed its rhythm to attract attention. Tomas does not know she is back.
4. Adrian financed the expedition using a promised exclusive claim on island discoveries. He has not told Elena; the claim puts the relay and islanders' work at risk. Kessler represents the claim holder, not a supernatural threat.
5. The team repairs the route to the tide room and finds Ines's signed plan. Adrian gives up the claim, and the crew drains the foot of the wall and makes the quay safe. When Mara tests the warning lamp, Ines waits and answers with the same three flashes from the safe landing. Tomas gives her the key, and she brings a chart of the other stations.

## Question and cast

The reply to Mara’s lamp poses the question **Who is signalling back?** The first two chapters find a person working the hand shutter. The third identifies Ines, the fourth finds the bypass she planned and the claim on it, and the fifth asks whether the crew can keep the quay safe without surrendering it.

| Person | Start → change → end | Voice and action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mara | Pilot chasing a flash → listens to an unfamiliar warning → carries Ines's message | Quick, funny, practical; her lamp is both safety gear and signal. |
| Elena | Scholar protecting evidence → trusts people over ownership → opens the records for the keepers | Precise, curious; tests her theories aloud and corrects them. |
| Ancient keeper | Leaves instructions for each new watch | The camp’s watch book preserves the keeper’s words and portrait. |
| Tomas | Guarded keeper → guides the search → shares the relay and its upkeep | Courteous and spare. He hands over the key to Ines’s cabinet. |
| Adrian | Charming sponsor with a secret claim → exposed → surrenders it to protect the quay | Deflects with wit; his signed claim is both danger and a choice. |
| Kessler | Distant claim holder's agent → presses for the records → loses the claim | Polite radio voice; no new portrait. |
| Marisol | Islander elder who runs the Jungle market (from 21) → tests Adrian, keeps a customer's secret → sees the quay jobs and records stay on the island | Shrewd, dry, protective of her cousins on the quay; trades in exact coin and better questions. |
| Ines | Absent signal sender → identified by note → answers from the safe landing | Acts through the relay and signed notes, then returns Tomas's trust with the chart. |

## Delivery and choices

Every first clear plays its expedition's scene; replays are silent. A story restoration's scene plays once it is placed. The Spring well brings Tomas to camp as a citizen, and the camp scene waits for that arrival. The Expedition camp opens the reply choice; later lines hold for either reply. On reload, an unread scene or selected reply resumes. Saves from the twenty-expedition script skip the scenes before their level and get a short recap.

## Chains and blue plaques

The blue plaques mark the crew’s work site. A chain holds a piece in place
until a match or tool breaks it. Blue plaques beneath chains take a separate
hit after the piece is free. See [board progression](board-progression.md) and the [asset catalogue](asset-catalogue.md#story-materials) for the art.

### Beat budgets

An expedition scene has two or three lines: the crew's delight at what they finished, then what to do next. Restoration scenes run longer (16, 61 and 66 most of all). Epoch openings (21, 41, 61, 81) and chapter closes (20, 40, 60, 80, 100) add a before-level scene. Ines speaks only at 100; Kessler is a radio voice.

## Chapter 1: A Light Below

**Level 1 · The Landing**

- **Mara:** Elena, hold the rope! The ring's all rust, but it kept my skiff, the Marigold, all night.
- **Elena:** Mara, look at the tide marks: green at my knee, white at my waist. Who measures a quay?
- **Adrian:** Water next, please, Elena. I fund wells; I don't dig them.

**After restoration 1 · Spring well**

- **Mara:** First bucket! Taste it, Elena. No salt, no rust, no goat.
- **Elena:** Sweet! And the rope brought up a scrap of harbour chart. Mara, quick, a flash by the cliff!
- **Mara:** Let's ask. Two flashes out... and two back! Elena, someone's down there.

**Level 2 · Jade Steps**

- **Elena:** Mara, working theory: your lamp hit wet jade and bounced off the cliff. A reflection.
- **Mara:** A reflection that waits its turn? Your jade has manners, Elena.
- **Elena:** A breath's delay is distance. Tonight my lamp stays in its sack. If it flashes, I'll eat the theory.

**Level 3 · The Ceiba Court**

- **Elena:** Midnight, lamp in its sack, and two flashes anyway. Mara, I withdraw the reflection.
- **Mara:** Put it in your notes, Elena: "Mara was right."
- **Elena:** Noted, in the margin, in pencil. Happy?

**Level 4 · Gate of Reeds**

- **Tomas:** Mind the third post; it bites. Tomas Reed. I keep this harbour.
- **Mara:** Keep it? They told us this harbour's been empty for years.
- **Tomas:** True; the islanders keep to the hill villages. Someone counts the tides. You mended my well.

**Level 5 · Trapper’s Terrace**

- **Elena:** Mr Reed, a lamp answered Mara from under the cliff. Whose is it?
- **Tomas:** Ines and I wired a warning lamp under that far cliff, Elena. The storm cut its cable.
- **Mara:** No cable, no lamp, Tomas. And it still winked at me twice. Rude.

**Level 6 · Rain Channel**

- **Tomas:** Come and see where the cable breaks. I spliced it by lantern, and the rain won.
- **Elena:** "Ines and I," you said before. Who is Ines?
- **Tomas:** She worked it with me. Another time, Elena. First, help me roof my father's cabin.

**Level 7 · The Timber Path**

- **Mara:** Tomas, the sawmill path! The roof fell in, but the big saw's still in its frame, rusty and smug.
- **Tomas:** Mend the workshop and it will cut again, Mara. Then I can build a reel for the cable.
- **Elena:** Then the workshop comes first, Tomas. Timber is the road to everything else here.

**Level 8 · Sun Court**

- **Elena:** Adrian, you had that radio talking a minute ago. Why is it in pieces?
- **Adrian:** Salt in the contacts, Elena. Everything out here dies of salt. Pass the screwdriver.
- **Mara:** I've watched salt eat a hinge overnight, Adrian. Believable. Almost.

**Level 9 · River Carvings**

- **Elena:** Hands off, Adrian! These carvings are older than your bank.
- **Adrian:** It leaned on me! What are they, Elena?
- **Elena:** A river, a tide, and a figure with a lamp at a shutter. Every watch had a keeper, Adrian.

**Level 10 · Tide Chamber**

- **Mara:** Elena, the third step is wet, and the stair runs on down under the inlet. Brave enough?
- **Elena:** Look at the walls, Mara: marks, one above another. Somebody measured this water for years.
- **Tomas:** It reaches the far station, Elena, and its tide room, the old vault. The key stays on my belt.

**Level 11 · Fallen Colonnade**

- **Mara:** Tomas, the colonnade fell like dominoes! Behind it: planks, dry as bread.
- **Tomas:** First good timber in years, Mara. Carry it to the workshop; the saw wants oil and time.
- **Adrian:** Wood! Tomas, at last, something on this island I can put on an invoice.

**Level 12 · Choked Gateway**

- **Tomas:** Mara, hold this: my first reel, shaped by hand. A drop of lamp oil and it turns.
- **Mara:** Tomas, it wobbles.
- **Tomas:** It will wind cable. Loose coils lie round the mooring posts, Mara. Bring them in.

**Level 13 · The Spire Keep**

- **Elena:** Tomas, from the spire I see a dark square under the far ledge. A shutter?
- **Tomas:** The far shutter. Shut since the storm, Elena. It should still be.
- **Mara:** Then someone forgot to tell it, Tomas.

**Level 14 · Hall of Pillars**

- **Elena:** Tomas, every pillar is a tide table, and every great storm is cut into the stone.
- **Adrian:** Elena, you're sure it's a table and not decoration? It's rather handsome.
- **Elena:** Decoration doesn't flood, Adrian. I'm sketching all of it.

**Level 15 · Broken Aqueduct**

- **Mara:** Elena, the aqueduct's broken in three places. So is the cable. The same three!
- **Elena:** Not coincidence, Mara. The cable ran along the arches, and the storm took both.
- **Tomas:** Then we wind from the first arch. Watch your feet, both of you.

**Level 16 · The Overlook**

- **Mara:** Elena, the harbour mouth, the far cliff, every wave I've ever sworn at, all from up here!
- **Elena:** And the dark shutter. Tomas, anyone down there would see a lamp from this height.
- **Tomas:** Then we need a camp with a proper watch: lamp oil, a book, someone awake. Help me build it.

**After restoration 16 · Expedition camp**

- **Mara:** Tomas, is that your father's watch book? Read me the first page.
- **Tomas:** Off the cabin shelf, Mara. The roof's on, the saw turns. First page is the keeper's hand.
- **Ancient keeper:** Whoever keeps this watch: when the tide covers the reef, open the shutter. Show the boats.
- **Mara:** Tomas, I flashed two from the lamp stand and three came back, with a pause before the third!
- **Tomas:** Ines kept the far shutter once, Mara. Nobody has, since the storm.
- **Elena:** Nobody, Tomas, and yet three flashes. Who should answer?

**Level 17 · Moss Bridge**

- **Mara:** Elena, one long flash at dusk, and nothing since. Moss has moved into my boots.
- **Elena:** Before, they always answered the same night, Mara. This silence is a message, too. They're thinking.
- **Tomas:** Or deciding whether we're friends. I'd wait, too, Elena.

**Level 18 · Twin Wells**

- **Elena:** Adrian, two wells side by side: one sweet, one brine. The sea reaches under this ridge.
- **Adrian:** Delightful. Here's the pump order, Elena, signed. I read the number; the number was charming.

**Level 19 · Inner Sun Court**

- **Elena:** Tomas, reflection is long dead. What's left is someone alive, with a lamp, who knows the old code.
- **Tomas:** Or someone taught by those who did, Elena.
- **Mara:** Then we'll be good company. Highest step tomorrow, at dusk. Coming, both of you?

**Before level 20**

- **Mara:** Highest step, dusk, tide over the reef, just as the book says. Elena, mind my lamp: it sticks.
- **Tomas:** Steady hands, Mara. We want them to know somebody is listening.
- **Elena:** One long flash, then we wait. Whatever comes, Mara, I'm ready.

**Level 20 · Heart of the Temple**

- **Mara:** Elena, our long flash crossed the gap, and a short one came back!
- **Elena:** Mara, mind the gaps between flashes: slow, uneven. That's no wire. That's a hand on a shutter.
- **Tomas:** Then somebody is standing in that dark room, on the wrong side of the water. How do we reach them?

## Chapter 2: Tracks on the Terrace

**Before level 21**

- **Mara:** Tomas, one long flash from us, a lopsided flash back. That's no relay, that's a wrist.
- **Elena:** A wrist on a schedule, Mara. Same count every night, never quite on time.
- **Tomas:** Follow the terrace path to that shutter, Mara. Loose stones everywhere; green arrows hold.

**Level 21 · Sun Disc**

- **Elena:** Tomas, the vines along this path are cut clean. Not torn, not rotted. Cut, and recently.
- **Mara:** A machete, or a very tidy goat, Elena.
- **Tomas:** The islanders know every track here. Give them a market, Mara, and they'll come back and talk.

**After restoration 21 · Jungle market**

- **Marisol:** So you're the lamp people. Auntie Marisol. The quay's work belongs to my people. Agreed?
- **Adrian:** In writing, madam, gladly. I adore a signature. And I'll buy every yard of your string.
- **Elena:** Marisol, the builders found a food bag behind your stall beam, dated last week. Yours?
- **Marisol:** My knot, my date. Who bought it is my business, scholar. Ask me better questions.

**Level 22 · Pilgrim Stair**

- **Mara:** Elena, whose boots? One pair up the stair, one pair back down. And don't say mine.
- **Elena:** Not yours, Mara. Short stride, left heel worn, someone smaller. Tomas, do you know that walk?
- **Tomas:** No. But a dated lunch and one pair of boots, Elena: somebody means to stay.

**Level 23 · Arch Gate**

- **Mara:** I'm fine! The lamp's fine! The moss and I will speak later.
- **Tomas:** You found the loose slab, Mara. The prints step round it. They knew it was there.
- **Elena:** Then someone has climbed this stair many times, Tomas.

**Level 24 · Temple Front**

- **Adrian:** Elena, my backers are the Meridian Society. Bankers with an interest in old harbours.
- **Elena:** Bankers on a dig, Adrian? What do they want?
- **Adrian:** Tidy reports, darling. Their man Kessler rings now and then. Excuse me, the ridge has signal.

**Level 25 · Star Dome**

- **Elena:** Tomas, nine replies logged since we landed. All at dusk or dawn, never at noon.
- **Mara:** A night shift, Elena. I respect that.
- **Tomas:** Dusk and dawn, when the tide covers the reef. The old order, kept to the letter, Elena.

**Level 26 · Cenote Ring**

- **Tomas:** Adrian, the quay wall is losing stones. It wants dressed stone, and the quarrymen sell at a post.
- **Adrian:** A trading post, then! Hand me the terms and a pen, Tomas. I never read, I trust faces.
- **Marisol:** Elena, take that pen off him. Paper decides my cousins' quay jobs, and he reads none.

**Level 27 · Colonnade Walk**

- **Mara:** Elena, new pattern! Long, short, short. Until now it was one lopsided flash.
- **Elena:** A mechanism repeats itself, Mara. A person who wants noticing changes the tune.
- **Tomas:** Then we answer politely, Mara. Whoever it is has waited a long while to be heard.

**Level 28 · Idol Head**

- **Elena:** Marisol, these prints stop at the water. Who buys a week of food from you, and gives no name?
- **Marisol:** Now that's a question. One customer, exact coin, and I date every bag so it keeps. No more.
- **Tomas:** Tides wipe prints, Elena. And nobody swims that gap. Not twice.

**Level 29 · Serpent Coil**

- **Tomas:** Mara, this lamp bracket was bent flat in the storm. Someone has hammered it straight.
- **Mara:** Right beside my lamp, and I never saw them? Tomas, that's showing off.
- **Elena:** Straightened, oiled, the lens wiped clean, Mara. A visitor who tidies. Hm.

**Level 30 · Sun Altar**

- **Mara (shout):** Red from the far lamp! Tomas, the reef's under the tide, dead ahead! Hauling the Marigold round!
- **Tomas:** My father's order, Mara, in red glass. Whoever it is keeps it better than I did.
- **Mara:** Soaked, alive, and in somebody's debt. Elena, I want to say thank you to a face.

**Level 31 · Beached Hull**

- **Mara:** Biscuit for breakfast, biscuit for lunch. Tomas, for one warm loaf I'd trade my lamp. Almost.
- **Elena:** Mill first, Mara. Meanwhile, my notes: dusk and dawn, only when the tide covers the reef.
- **Tomas:** Hours follow the water, Elena. My father flashed one for each hour of the rise.

**Level 32 · Lantern Tower**

- **Tomas:** Elena, the wire ran down the stair to the tide room at the far station. I bolted it.
- **Mara:** Then who gets in to work a shutter, Tomas? High water floods that stair.
- **Elena:** Someone who crosses at low water, Mara, and waits on the ledge as it rises. How, I can't yet say.

**Level 33 · Terrace Garden**

- **Elena:** Adrian, theory: the Meridian Society wants treasure, and Kessler's men are scouting ahead of us.
- **Adrian:** Kessler's men? Dull little man, Elena. He sends lawyers, never anyone who can climb a stair.
- **Mara:** Scouts who answer my lamp, Adrian? Very polite, for treasure hunters.

**Level 34 · Water Cistern**

- **Tomas:** Treasure hunters take, Elena. The brass bell is still on its hook, and the lens whole.
- **Elena:** Then Kessler's men are struck from the notebook, Tomas.
- **Mara:** A burglar who tidies up. Elena, I think we have a colleague.

**Level 35 · Jaguar Gate**

- **Mara:** Tomas, I stood there two seconds ago! A stone the size of a stool stands there now.
- **Tomas:** The quay wall, Mara: a crack has opened behind it, and three stones are down. Which hours are safe?
- **Elena:** The old tide clock would tell us, Tomas, if it had a tower, hands and a dial.

**Level 36 · Moss Well**

- **Mara:** Elena, I fished a brass disc out of the moss well. Heavy, green, covered in numbers.
- **Elena:** Mara, a tide dial, marked for this very harbour! In a tower it would tell us the safe hours.
- **Tomas:** Then give it a tower, Elena. My father set his whole day by that clock.

**After restoration 36 · Tide clock tower**

- **Mara:** Elena, the clock ticks! Which hours can I walk the quay without a stone on my head?
- **Elena:** Green hours are safe, Mara; red hours, stay off. Tomas, why does the red band begin there?
- **Tomas:** It marks the crack's height, Elena. The crack runs into the quay wall, and the sea comes through it.
- **Elena:** And the flashes come as the red hours begin, Tomas. Whoever it is keeps the water's hours.

**Level 37 · Sunken Plaza**

- **Mara:** Tomas, we walked the wall's foot in the green hours and found a rope, knotted for climbing.
- **Elena:** Fresh hemp, new knots, Tomas. Whoever climbs here expects the tide back soon.
- **Tomas:** It's tied to a ring I set myself, years ago. Trust the loops, Mara. They hold.

**Level 38 · Stone Bridge**

- **Tomas:** Adrian, the strongroom keeps the old bearings. It stays shut until the plaza is mended.
- **Adrian:** Then open it yesterday, Tomas! I'm dying to see an old bearing.
- **Marisol:** Look all you like, Mr Vale. The island's records stay where the island can reach them.

**Level 39 · Temple of Winds**

- **Elena:** Mara, hand me Adrian's string. This crack's an arm's length wide, so he's useful at last.
- **Mara:** Hero of the market, Elena! I'll tell him.
- **Tomas:** It was a finger's width when I shut the tide room, Elena. The spring tides have opened it.

**Before level 40**

- **Elena:** Tomas, the green hours open soon. Help me trace this crack to the end of the wall.
- **Mara:** And I want to meet whoever tied those knots, Elena. Both, then. Let's walk.
- **Tomas:** Slowly, Mara. The wall is older than my patience, and in a worse mood.

**Level 40 · Serpent Throne**

- **Elena:** Mara, Tomas, look: pencil fresh under this stone. "Wall unsafe. I. R." It follows the crack.
- **Marisol:** My cousins who work this quay pass that wall daily. I'll warn them today: short, polite, right.
- **Tomas:** I. R. I know that R, Elena. The harbour register will tell us, once the plaza is open.

## Chapter 3: The Keeper's Return

**Before level 41**

- **Elena:** Tomas, pencil on stone: a warning for the quay, two initials. Someone measured this wall.
- **Mara:** A stranger with manners, flashing at us from a room the sea floods. Tomas, who is she?
- **Tomas:** Every boat signed the harbour register, Mara. Find it, and I'll know that R.

**Level 41 · Reed Hut**

- **Mara:** Elena, under the reed hut's floor: a book the size of a door. It smells like a wet goat.
- **Elena:** The harbour register! Tomas, every boat that ever tied up signed in ink.
- **Tomas:** It wants a dry table, Elena. Open the plaza and I'll read it to you.

**After restoration 41 · Harbour plaza**

- **Tomas:** Elena, page nine, over and over: that R like a fishhook. Ines Reed. My sister.
- **Mara:** Reed, as in Tomas Reed? As in "Ines and I wired it"? You dark horse.
- **Tomas:** I should have said so, Mara. After the storm, her name got hard to say.
- **Marisol:** Tomas, my bag buyer is a woman who never gave a name. She asked for quiet. I gave it.
- **Elena:** Her last entry is the storm night, Marisol, yet the stone's note is this week's. Who is she?

**Level 42 · Tide Pool**

- **Elena:** Tomas, forgive me, but I need the true order of that night.
- **Tomas:** The wall cracked, so I closed the tide room. Ines left for the plans. That was all.
- **Mara:** And you stayed to mind the lamp, Tomas. Of course you did.

**Level 43 · Lookout Mesa**

- **Tomas:** Then the storm cut the cable, Elena. I spliced until dawn and I did it alone.
- **Elena:** And Ines?
- **Tomas:** She never came up the cliff road. I kept the arrows painted green and watched it anyway.

**Level 44 · Bone Stair**

- **Tomas:** Elena, I've thought it through. Ines must have drowned, and someone is borrowing her initials.
- **Elena:** It fits what we hold, Tomas. I do not like how neatly it fits.
- **Mara:** A stranger who signs with her fishhook R? Then let's find a second sample of that hand, Elena.

**Level 45 · Vault Door**

- **Elena:** Tomas, a tide table's chalked inside the vault's stair door, this week's tides ticked in pencil.
- **Tomas:** Hooked ticks, Elena. I taught that tally to one person. She added the hook to annoy me.
- **Tomas:** I was wrong, both of you. Nobody borrows a tally. Ines is alive, and she is writing.

**Level 46 · Dawn Terrace**

- **Mara:** Dawn, and the far shutter's still winking, Elena. That woman never sleeps.
- **Elena:** Whatever papers she left need more than a windy terrace. We need one hall, one table, Adrian.
- **Adrian:** I volunteer to carry chairs, Elena. I'm told it's the one thing I do well.

**After restoration 46 · Harbour lodge**

- **Mara:** Adrian, that's my elbow! One table for the whole crew, and red mullet for twelve.
- **Tomas:** Elena, Ines's harbour log, dry on the lodge shelf. The crack grows a finger each spring tide.
- **Elena:** Tomas, page three says "bypass", with no drawing, and the last page is torn out. What did she mean?
- **Tomas:** I don't know, Elena. Ines never explained a thing until it worked.

**Level 47 · Salt Stair**

- **Mara:** Elena, a page stuck in the salt crust on the steps. Same pencil as the tally!
- **Elena:** "Six feet," underlined twice, Tomas. "Plans in the chart cabinet, no." Then it tears off.
- **Tomas:** Six feet from what, Elena? And a cabinet without a number.

**Level 48 · Jaguar Lintel**

- **Tomas:** Elena, the stone gauge by the lantern steps. She took every reading there.
- **Mara:** Tomas, it's under a snarling jaguar and a metre of ferns. It looks pleased to be found.
- **Elena:** Clear it, both of you. If she measured six feet, she'll have left a mark.

**Level 49 · Drowned Arch**

## Small lines

**The delayed reply.** Mara counts two flashes at the Spring well and a delayed three from the near shore. ## Checks and implementation notes

The scenes play over the live island with the speaking character’s portrait. The keeper’s words come from the watch book in the camp scene. `tools/prepare_story.py` compiles this script into `lua/story_content.lua`; it requires a scene after every expedition and one at each story restoration. Five writers drafted one chapter each from a shared outline; a continuity editor reconciled them (the cabinet number, the key, the register, Ines's reveal, who knows what when) and a separate cold reader read the result chapter by chapter.
