# Relic Rush: A Light Below

**Status:** One hundred expeditions in five chapters of twenty, one per epoch of four restorations. Every expedition has a scene on its first clear; the ten story restorations have a scene when they are placed. The reply choice is at the Expedition camp (16).

**Play fantasy:** Bring a working harbour back to life with a present-day expedition crew and find the keeper signalling from under the cliff. The feeling is curious, warm-hearted pulp adventure. Each repair gives the crew something they can use on the next leg of the search.

## What is broken

The camp is abandoned, the old well is fouled, and the quay wall is failing. The broken electric relay leaves the lower quay without its regular warning. Ines warns boats by hand across a flooded gap. The crew needs water, timber and access to the harbour register and the keeper's archive.

## Truth timeline

1. The original harbor builders built the lower quay and fitted a hand shutter to warn boats away from the reef. Generations later, Tomas and his sister Ines fitted an electric relay. A lamp at the old lookout can answer someone at the warning shutter.
2. Tomas and Ines worked together. When the quay wall cracked, Tomas closed the room; Ines left to fetch repair plans and supplies. Tomas promised to maintain the warning, but a storm cut the cable. He tried to splice it and left his pliers at the break. He believes Ines did not return.
3. Recently Ines returned by a different shore. She found the room flooded and, unable to reach camp, used its hand shutter to send the old warning. She changed its rhythm to attract attention. Tomas does not know she is back.
4. Adrian financed the expedition using a promised exclusive claim on island discoveries. He has not told Elena; the claim puts the relay and islanders' work at risk. Kessler represents the claim holder, not a supernatural threat.
5. The team repairs the route to the tide room and finds Ines's signed plan. Adrian gives up the claim, and the crew drains the foot of the wall and makes the quay safe. When Mara tests the warning lamp, Ines waits and answers with the same three flashes from the safe landing. Tomas gives her the key, and she brings a chart of the other stations.

## Question and cast

The reply to Mara’s lamp poses the question **Who is signalling back?** The first two chapters find a person working the hand shutter. The third identifies Ines, the fourth finds the bypass she planned and the claim on it, and the fifth asks whether the crew can keep the quay safe without surrendering it.

| Person | Start → change → end | Voice and action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mara | Pilot chasing a flash → listens to an unfamiliar warning → carries Ines's message | Quick, funny, practical; her lamp is both safety gear and signal. |
| Elena | Scholar protecting evidence → trusts people over ownership → opens the records for the keepers | Precise, curious; tests her theories aloud and corrects them. |
| Ancient keeper | Leaves instructions for each new watch | The camp’s watch book preserves the keeper’s words and portrait. |
| Tomas | Guarded keeper → guides the search → shares the relay and its upkeep | Courteous and spare. He hands over the key to Ines’s cabinet. |
| Adrian | Charming sponsor with a secret claim → exposed → surrenders it to protect the quay | Deflects with wit; his signed claim is both danger and a choice. |
| Kessler | Distant claim holder's agent → presses for the records → loses the claim | Polite radio voice; no new portrait. |
| Ines | Absent signal sender → identified by note → answers from the safe landing | Acts through the relay and signed notes, then returns Tomas's trust with the chart. |

## Delivery and choices

Every first clear plays its expedition's scene; replays are silent. A story restoration's scene plays once it is placed. The Spring well brings Tomas to camp as a citizen, and the camp scene waits for that arrival. The Expedition camp opens the reply choice; later lines hold for either reply. The separate hand-shutter test opens on the cable hand-in. On reload, an unread scene or selected reply resumes. Saves from the twenty-expedition script skip the scenes before their level and get a short recap.

## Chains and blue plaques

The blue plaques mark the crew’s work site. A chain holds a piece in place
until a match or tool breaks it. Blue plaques beneath chains take a separate
hit after the piece is free. See [board progression](board-progression.md) and the [asset catalogue](asset-catalogue.md#story-materials) for the art.

### Beat budgets

Every scene has two or three lines: the crew's delight at what they finished, then what to do next. Epoch openings (21, 41, 61, 81) and chapter closes (20, 40, 60, 80, 100) add a before-level scene. Ines speaks only at 99 and 100; Kessler is a radio voice.

## Chapter 1: A Light Below

**Level 1 · The Landing**

- **Mara:** Landing's clear. The mooring ring is all rust, but the rope held, and so did the Marigold, my skiff.
- **Elena:** Tide marks up the quay: green at my knee, white at my waist. Someone watched this water.
- **Adrian:** Splendid. Water next, please. I fund wells; I don't dig them.

**After restoration 1 · Spring well**

- **Mara:** First bucket! Taste it, Elena. No salt, no rust, no goat.
- **Elena:** Sweet. And the rope brought up a scrap of harbour chart. Wait, a flash by the cliff!
- **Mara:** Let's ask. Two flashes out... and two back! Someone's down there.

**Level 2 · Jade Steps**

- **Elena:** Working theory: Mara's lamp hit wet jade and bounced off the cliff. A reflection.
- **Mara:** A reflection that waits its turn? Your jade has manners.
- **Elena:** A breath's delay is distance. Tonight my lamp stays in its sack. If it flashes, I'll eat the theory.

**Level 3 · The Ceiba Court**

- **Elena:** Midnight, lamp in its sack, ceiba roots at my back, and two flashes anyway. Reflection withdrawn.
- **Mara:** Put it in your notes: "Mara was right."
- **Elena:** Noted. In the margin, in pencil.

**Level 4 · Gate of Reeds**

- **Tomas:** Mind the third post; it bites. Tomas Reed. I keep this harbour.
- **Mara:** Keep it? We were told the place has been empty for years.
- **Tomas:** It has. Someone still has to count the tides. You mended my well, I see. Thank you.

**Level 5 · Trapper’s Terrace**

- **Elena:** Mr Reed, a lamp answered Mara from under the cliff. Whose is it?
- **Tomas:** Ines and I wired a warning lamp under that far cliff. The storm cut its cable.
- **Mara:** No cable, no lamp. And it still winked at me twice. Rude.

**Level 6 · Rain Channel**

- **Tomas:** The cable ends at a break up this channel. I spliced it by lantern. The rain won.
- **Elena:** "Ines and I," you said. Who is Ines?
- **Tomas:** She worked it with me. Another time. First, my father's cabin needs a roof.

**Level 7 · The Timber Path**

- **Mara:** Sawmill path! The roof fell in, but the big saw is still in its frame, rusty and smug.
- **Tomas:** Once the workshop is mended, it will cut again. Then I can build a reel for the cable.
- **Elena:** Then the workshop comes first. Timber is the road to everything else here.

**Level 8 · Sun Court**

- **Elena:** Adrian had the radio talking a minute ago. Now it's in pieces on the paving.
- **Adrian:** Salt in the contacts. Everything out here dies of salt. Pass the screwdriver.
- **Mara:** I've watched salt eat a hinge overnight. Believable.

**Level 9 · River Carvings**

- **Elena:** Hands off, Adrian! These carvings are older than your bank.
- **Adrian:** It leaned on me. What are they?
- **Elena:** A river, a tide, and a figure with a lamp at a shutter. Every watch had a keeper.

**Level 10 · Tide Chamber**

- **Mara:** Tide chamber! Water's licking the third step, and the stair runs on down under the inlet.
- **Elena:** Marks on every wall, one above another. Somebody measured this water for years.
- **Tomas:** It reaches the far station. I locked the room at its end myself. The key stays on my belt.

**Level 11 · Fallen Colonnade**

- **Mara:** The colonnade fell like dominoes. Behind it: planks, dry as bread.
- **Tomas:** Good timber, the first I've seen in years. The workshop's saw wants oil and time.
- **Adrian:** Wood! At last, something on this island I can put on an invoice.

**Level 12 · Choked Gateway**

- **Tomas:** First timber, first reel, shaped by hand. A drop of lamp oil on the spindle and it turns.
- **Mara:** It wobbles.
- **Tomas:** It will wind cable. Loose coils lie round the mooring posts. Bring them in.

**Level 13 · The Spire Keep**

- **Elena:** From the spire I can see the far cliff. A dark square under the ledge. A shutter?
- **Tomas:** The far shutter. It has been shut since the storm. It should still be.
- **Mara:** Then someone forgot to tell it.

**Level 14 · Hall of Pillars**

- **Elena:** Every pillar is a tide table, and every great storm is cut into the stone.
- **Adrian:** Are there copies of these? Somewhere off the island, say. Safer, surely.
- **Elena:** Stone doesn't copy itself. I'm sketching all of it.

**Level 15 · Broken Aqueduct**

- **Mara:** The aqueduct's broken in three places. So is the cable. The same three.
- **Elena:** Not coincidence. The cable ran along the arches, and the storm took both.
- **Tomas:** Then we wind from the first arch. Watch your feet.

**Level 16 · The Overlook**

- **Mara:** The Overlook! The harbour mouth, the far cliff, and every wave I've ever sworn at.
- **Elena:** And the dark shutter. If anyone's down there, they'll see a lamp from here.
- **Tomas:** Then we want a camp with a proper watch. Lamp oil, a book, someone awake.

**After restoration 16 · Expedition camp**

- **Mara:** Camp's up and the night watch is posted. Is that your father's watch book, Tomas?
- **Tomas:** Off the cabin shelf. The roof is on, the workshop saw turns. First page is the keeper's.
- **Ancient keeper:** When the tide covers the reef, open the warning shutter. Let the boats see the light.
- **Mara:** I flashed two from the lamp stand. Three came back, a pause before the third!
- **Tomas:** Ines kept the far shutter once. Nobody has, since the storm.
- **Elena:** Nobody. And yet three flashes. Who should answer?

Choice prompt: Who should answer the signal?

### Choice: mara | Ask Mara to reply

- **Mara:** One long flash, so they know we've seen them.
- **Tomas:** Keep it steady. There. They'll know somebody's on watch.

### Choice: tomas | Ask Tomas to reply

- **Tomas:** One long flash means we are listening. Let me show you.
- **Mara:** Here. Take my lamp. It sticks if you try to be gentle.

**Level 17 · Moss Bridge**

- **Mara:** One long flash went out at dusk. Nothing since. Moss has moved into my boots.
- **Elena:** Before, they answered at once. This silence is a message too. They're thinking.
- **Tomas:** Or deciding whether we're friends. I'd wait, too.

**Level 18 · Twin Wells**

- **Elena:** Two wells side by side: one sweet, one brine. The sea reaches under this ridge.
- **Adrian:** Delightful. Here's the pump order, signed. I read the number; the number was charming.
- **Elena:** The rest of the page exists, Adrian. Someday you must try it.

**Level 19 · Inner Sun Court**

- **Elena:** Reflection is long dead. What's left: someone alive, with a lamp, who knows the old code.
- **Tomas:** Or someone who learned it from those who did.
- **Mara:** Then we'll be good company. Highest step tomorrow, at dusk.

**Before level 20**

- **Mara:** Highest step, dusk. The tide is covering the reef, just as the book says. Mind my lamp: it sticks.
- **Tomas:** Steady hands, then. We want them to know somebody is listening.
- **Elena:** Our long flash, then we wait. Whatever comes, I'm writing it down.

**Level 20 · Heart of the Temple**

- **Mara:** Our long flash crosses the gap... and a short one comes back!
- **Elena:** Mind the gaps between flashes: slow, uneven. That's no wire. That's a hand on a shutter.
- **Tomas:** Then somebody is standing in that dark room, on the wrong side of the water.

## Chapter 2: Tracks on the Terrace

**Before level 21**

- **Mara:** One long flash from our lamp. A lopsided flash back. That's no relay, that's a wrist.
- **Elena:** A wrist on a schedule. Same count every night, never quite on time. I'm writing it down.
- **Tomas:** The terrace path runs straight at that shutter. Mind the loose stones. Green arrows hold.

**Level 21 · Sun Disc**

- **Elena:** The vines along the path are cut clean. Not torn, not rotted. Cut, and recently.
- **Mara:** A machete, or a very tidy goat.
- **Tomas:** The islanders know every track here. Give them a market and they'll come back.

**After restoration 21 · Jungle market**

- **Mara:** Market's open, and the islanders who work the quay are back! Adrian bought all the string.
- **Elena:** The builders found this behind a stall beam: a sealed food bag, dated last week.
- **Tomas:** Dated, dry, and hidden from the damp. Somebody is out here, and means to stay.
- **Adrian:** I like them already. Anyone who dates their lunch has my full respect.

**Level 22 · Pilgrim Stair**

- **Mara:** Boot prints in the wet clay. One pair going up the stair, one pair coming down.
- **Elena:** One person. Short stride, left heel worn. Smaller than you, Mara.
- **Mara:** Rude, and probably correct. Keep walking.

**Level 23 · Arch Gate**

- **Mara:** I'm fine. The lamp's fine. The moss and I will speak later.
- **Tomas:** You found the loose slab. The prints step round it. They knew it was there.
- **Elena:** Then someone who has climbed this stair before. Many times. Noted.

**Level 24 · Temple Front**

- **Adrian:** Elena, my backers are the Meridian Society. Bankers with an interest in old harbours.
- **Elena:** Bankers on a dig. What do they want?
- **Adrian:** Tidy reports. Their man Kessler rings now and then. Excuse me, the ridge has the signal.

**Level 25 · Star Dome**

- **Elena:** Nine replies logged since we landed. All at dusk or dawn. Never at noon.
- **Mara:** A night shift. I respect that.
- **Tomas:** Dusk and dawn, when the tide covers the reef. The old order, kept to the letter.

**Level 26 · Cenote Ring**

- **Tomas:** The quay wall is losing stones. It wants dressed stone, and the quarrymen sell to a post.
- **Adrian:** A trading post, then! Hand me the terms and a pen. I never read, I trust faces.
- **Elena:** That is the least comforting sentence I have heard all week.

**Level 27 · Colonnade Walk**

- **Mara:** New pattern! Long, short, short. Until now it was three, with a pause before the third.
- **Elena:** A mechanism repeats itself. A person who wants noticing changes the tune.
- **Tomas:** Then we answer politely. Whoever it is has waited a long while to be heard.

**Level 28 · Idol Head**

- **Mara:** The prints run down to the shore and stop dead at the water's edge.
- **Elena:** Tides wipe prints. But where does a person go from here?
- **Tomas:** Not swimming. Nobody swims that gap. Not twice.

**Level 29 · Serpent Coil**

- **Tomas:** This lamp bracket was bent flat in the storm. Someone has hammered it straight.
- **Mara:** At our lookout? Right beside my lamp, and I never saw them.
- **Elena:** Straightened, oiled, the lens wiped clean. A visitor who tidies. Hm.

**Level 30 · Sun Altar**

- **Mara (shout):** Red from the far lamp! The reef's under the tide, dead ahead! Hauling the Marigold round!
- **Tomas:** My father's order, kept in red glass. Whoever it is keeps it better than I did.
- **Mara:** Soaked, alive, and in somebody's debt. I want to say thank you to a face.

**Level 31 · Beached Hull**

- **Mara:** Biscuit for breakfast, biscuit for lunch. For one warm loaf I'd trade my lamp. Almost.
- **Elena:** The mill wants repairs first. My notes: dusk and dawn, but only when the tide covers the reef.
- **Tomas:** Hours follow the water. My father flashed one for each hour of the rise.

**Level 32 · Lantern Tower**

- **Tomas:** The wire ran down the stair to the tide room under the far cliff. I bolted it.
- **Mara:** Then who gets in to work a shutter? High water floods that stair.
- **Elena:** Someone who crosses at low water and waits on the ledge as it rises. How, I don't yet know.

**Level 33 · Terrace Garden**

- **Elena:** Theory. The Meridian Society wants treasure, and Kessler's men are scouting ahead of us.
- **Adrian:** Kessler? Dull little man. He sends lawyers, never anyone who can climb a stair.
- **Mara:** Scouts who answer my lamp. Very polite, for treasure hunters.

**Level 34 · Water Cistern**

- **Tomas:** Treasure hunters take. The brass bell is still on its hook, and the lens still whole.
- **Elena:** Kessler's men: withdrawn. Struck from the notebook.
- **Mara:** A burglar who tidies up. I think we have a colleague.

**Level 35 · Jaguar Gate**

- **Mara:** I was standing there two seconds ago. A stone the size of a stool is standing there now.
- **Tomas:** The quay wall. Three stones down this month. We need to know which hours are safe.
- **Elena:** The old tide clock would tell us. If it had a tower, and hands, and a dial.

**Level 36 · Moss Well**

- **Mara:** Fished a brass disc out of the moss well. Heavy, green, covered in numbers.
- **Elena:** A tide dial, marked for this very harbour! In a tower it would tell us the safe hours.
- **Tomas:** Then give it a tower. My father set his whole day by that clock.

**After restoration 36 · Tide clock tower**

- **Mara:** The tide clock ticks! Mill bread in my pocket, quarry stone coming in. Gloriously busy.
- **Elena:** Green hours, safe on the quay. Red hours, stay off. Look where the red band begins.
- **Tomas:** That is the crack's height. It runs into the quay wall, and the sea comes through it.
- **Elena:** And the flashes come as the red hours begin. Whoever it is keeps the water's hours.

**Level 37 · Sunken Plaza**

- **Mara:** Green hours, so we walked the foot of the wall. Look: a rope, knotted for climbing.
- **Elena:** Fresh hemp, new knots. Whoever climbs here expects the tide back soon.
- **Tomas:** Tied to a ring I set myself, years ago. Mind the loops. They hold.

**Level 38 · Stone Bridge**

- **Tomas:** The harbour strongroom keeps the old bearings. It stays shut until the plaza is mended.
- **Mara:** Adrian alphabetises his socks, Tomas. He'll want it open yesterday.
- **Adrian:** Guilty. I like a tidy shelf.

**Level 39 · Temple of Winds**

- **Elena:** The crack is an arm's length wide. Adrian's string, at last, is useful.
- **Mara:** Hero of the market. I'll tell him.
- **Tomas:** A finger's width when I shut the tide room. The spring tides have opened it.

**Before level 40**

- **Elena:** The green hours open soon. I want this crack traced to the end of the wall.
- **Mara:** And I want to meet whoever tied those knots. Both, then. Let's walk.
- **Tomas:** Slowly. The wall is older than my patience, and in a worse mood.

**Level 40 · Serpent Throne**

- **Elena:** A note under this stone, pencil fresh: “Wall unsafe. I. R.” The crack runs through the wall.
- **Mara:** The islanders who work the quay pass this wall daily. Tell them today. Short, polite, right.
- **Tomas:** I. R. I know that R. The harbour register will tell us, once the plaza is open.

## Chapter 3: The Keeper's Return

**Before level 41**

- **Elena:** Pencil on stone: a warning for the quay, signed with two initials. Someone measured this wall.
- **Mara:** A stranger with manners, flashing at us from a room the sea floods.
- **Tomas:** Every boat signed the harbour register. Find it, and I'll know that R.

**Level 41 · Reed Hut**

- **Mara:** Under the reed hut's floor: a book the size of a door. It smells like a wet goat.
- **Elena:** The harbour register! Every boat that ever tied up signed in ink.
- **Tomas:** It wants a dry table. Open the plaza and I'll read it to you.

**After restoration 41 · Harbour plaza**

- **Tomas:** Page nine, over and over: that R like a fishhook. Ines Reed. My sister.
- **Mara:** Reed. As in Tomas Reed. As in "Ines and I wired it", you dark horse.
- **Tomas:** I should have said so. After the storm her name got hard to say.
- **Elena:** Her last entry is the night of the storm. The note under the stone is this week's.

**Level 42 · Tide Pool**

- **Elena:** Tomas, forgive me, but I need the true order of that night.
- **Tomas:** The wall cracked, so I closed the tide room. Ines left for the plans. That was all.
- **Mara:** And you stayed to mind the lamp. Of course you did.

**Level 43 · Lookout Mesa**

- **Tomas:** Then the storm cut the cable. I spliced until dawn and left my pliers at the break.
- **Elena:** And Ines?
- **Tomas:** She never came up the cliff road. I kept the arrows painted green and watched it anyway.

**Level 44 · Bone Stair**

- **Tomas:** I have thought it through. Ines must have drowned, and someone is borrowing her initials.
- **Elena:** It fits what we hold. I do not like how neatly it fits.
- **Mara:** A stranger who signs with her fishhook R? Let's find a second sample of that hand.

**Level 45 · Vault Door**

- **Elena:** A tide table chalked inside the vault door, and this week's tides ticked off in pencil.
- **Tomas:** Hooked ticks. I taught that tally to one person. She added the hook to annoy me.
- **Tomas:** I was wrong. Nobody borrows a tally. Ines is alive, and she is writing.

**Level 46 · Dawn Terrace**

- **Mara:** Dawn, and the far shutter is still winking. That woman never sleeps.
- **Elena:** Whatever papers she left need more than a windy terrace. One hall, one table.
- **Adrian:** I volunteer to carry chairs. I'm told it's the one thing I do well.

**After restoration 46 · Harbour lodge**

- **Mara:** One table for the whole crew, and red mullet for twelve. Adrian, that's my elbow.
- **Tomas:** Ines's harbour log, dry on the lodge shelf. The crack grows a finger each spring tide.
- **Elena:** Page three says "bypass", with no drawing. The last page is torn out.

**Level 47 · Salt Stair**

- **Mara:** A page stuck in the salt crust on the steps. Same pencil as the tally!
- **Elena:** "Six feet," underlined twice. "Plans in the chart cabinet, no." Then it tears off.
- **Tomas:** Six feet from what? And a cabinet without a number.

**Level 48 · Jaguar Lintel**

- **Tomas:** The stone gauge by the lantern steps. She took every reading there.
- **Mara:** It's under a snarling jaguar and a metre of ferns. It looks pleased to be found.
- **Elena:** Clear it. If she measured six feet, she'll have left a mark.

**Level 49 · Drowned Arch**

- **Elena:** There's the six-foot notch, deep as a thumb. And a 7 cut right beside it.
- **Tomas:** A gauge reads tides. It has no use for a 7. I never cut that.
- **Mara:** Then it's her handwriting, in stone. What is she counting?

**Level 50 · Idol of Rain**

- **Mara:** Rain on the quay, and the crack is spitting water like a cross whale.
- **Tomas:** A finger wider than her log says. We have less time than the book promised.
- **Elena:** Then we find her plan before the next spring tide.

**Level 51 · Mole Walk**

- **Kessler:** Good evening, Mr Vale. I hear a log has turned up. I should like the records.
- **Adrian:** Terrible line, Kessler! Wind off the sea. I'm losing you. Losing you entirely.
- **Mara:** The sea is flat as a plate, Adrian.

**Level 52 · Kelp Terrace**

- **Elena:** The islanders want somewhere to sit and argue. The amphitheatre is being mended for them.
- **Tomas:** I told them about Ines tonight, at the masons' fire. I said her name, and nothing fell.
- **Mara:** And the hut lamp burns every night now. For the boats, you tell me.

**Level 53 · Ceiba Roots**

- **Elena:** Rereading her log. Water pushes at the foot of the wall, never the top.
- **Mara:** So you give the sea a detour. A bypass! A scenic route.
- **Tomas:** Ines drew circles in the sand when she was thinking. I never asked what they were.

**Level 54 · Tide Stairs**

- **Mara:** Tonight's answer came slower: long, long, short. She never sends the same twice.
- **Elena:** Then she isn't a fault repeating. She's talking, and we're slow to learn the language.
- **Tomas:** I'm looking, Ines.

**Level 55 · Signal Tower**

- **Mara:** Far cliff, tide room door, half under water. And a bucket hung on the ledge.
- **Elena:** Hung out to dry. Someone bails that room by hand, and keeps a routine.

## Crew requests

Between two purchases the crew asks for smaller things. A request puts one piece of its own on the expedition boards. It banks nothing in the wallet. Matching it fills the request across expeditions, and the open requests stand as round markers down the island's left edge, under the next building. Handing a filled request in plays its scene. [Request data](../lua/quests.lua) · [Board rules](match4-rules.md#crew-requests)

Two lines run side by side. **Search** follows the light: Mara and the keeper's old route. **Crew** is about the people at the table. A request opens once its building stands and the one before it on its line is handed in. The cable hand-in opens a field test at the lookout. A player may hand requests in even after the campaign ends. Each scene therefore holds on its own: it names nothing that only a later restoration establishes, and no restoration scene relies on it.

- **Mara:** The last one is this year's paint.

- **Mara:** There's an empty loop in the roll.
- **Tomas:** The pliers. I left them at the break in the cable, the night of the storm.

- **Mara:** There's the break. And a pair of pliers clamped on it.
- **Tomas:** Mine. Rusted shut. Nothing has run through this wire since the storm.
- **Mara:** If the wire's dead, what would a boat offshore have seen?
- **Tomas:** I've a spare shutter for the lookout lamp. Take your skiff out and I'll show you.

- **Mara:** Red! The rocks are dead ahead of me.

- **Mara:** White now. And there's clear water over my bow.

A cold reader followed all five. They found four lines that broke when a request came late: a question Tomas had already answered, a tide explained after the crew had used it, pliers one scene assumed from another, and a closing inference that fell flat after the three flashes. Those lines now state a fact of their own. The reader judged that the cable scene strengthens the reply at the Expedition camp.

A cold reader tested both scene orders and caught an ambiguity in the experiment. Tomas holds the ring steady while Mara rows across the bearing. Red changes to white because the boat moves. Afterward the Carpenter’s workshop retains Elena’s finding in its inspection text.

The spare is a test rig at the near lookout. The Expedition camp scene can still exchange flashes across the gap. Ines and the far station are never required for the skiff test. The experiment can happen before that exchange, after it, or after the finale.

## Small lines

**The delayed reply.** Mara counts two flashes at the Spring well and a delayed three from the near shore. On board 100, the signal plate appears after the last blue plaque is removed, and Ines answers with the same three flashes from the safe landing. With reduced motion, the lamp holds steady and the dialogue names the pattern.
