# Relic Rush · A Crust Each Way

**Campaign proposal for review.** In an ancient island port, a potter wants her daughter to grow up in the house where she grew up. Rebuild the town with her, from the first bucket of sweet water to the morning two neighbourhoods can bake bread at once.

## The first bucket

**Before level 1 · The landing**

- **Sela:** Can I leave Ina's sleeping mat at your house?
- **Oren:** Roll it up again. By noon she'll be thirsty and you'll have to row her back.
- **Sela:** I came home to stay, Oren. Lend me the bucket.

**After restoration 1 · Spring well**

- **Oren:** Give me the first taste. I drank from here the day the sea came in.
- **Sela:** Well?
- **Oren:** Sweet all the way down. Where did you put her mat?

That evening Sela has to carry Ina upstairs for the night. Down by the boats, the public spout is dry. The well on the upper terrace has brought one family home. Getting water to the quay will take a city.

## The city and its people

This is a fictional island in antiquity. Grain comes by sail. Potters fire short clay pipes that can be carried on a shoulder. Neighbours settle a debt before witnesses in the sun court. The city's history fits inside a family. People remember the flood, who fed them through it and who still has to climb for water.

| Speaker | What each has at stake |
| --- | --- |
| Sela, a potter | Wants to reopen her mother's kiln and live by it with Ina. She can make a pipe and trace its joints by touch. Her mistake is promising the quay water that somebody upstream already depends on. |
| Oren, her elder brother and the miller | Kept people fed after the flood. He owes grain to six households who lent him seed. A morning without the millwheel turning costs him a day at the handstone. He makes Sela come and grind beside him when her first plan fails. |
| Tavi, a ferry pilot | Carries water downhill in the morning and fares across the inlet in the afternoon. She could earn more carrying jars forever. She has promised Ina a morning at the oars once the water run is finished. She can tell from a wet mark on a landing step whether her ferry will clear the bar. |

Sela and Oren open the game. Tavi first speaks at the Jungle market. Ina remains a child in their care. The same three adults carry the story through the finale.

## What happened before the player arrived

1. A hillside spring once fed the lower quarter through a clay conduit. The shore households drew from a common basin.
2. Last winter a storm tide took the mill dam and cracked the lower conduit. Families moved inland. Oren and his mother borrowed seed grain to feed the displaced families. He hauled the sacks. She sealed the loan. He patched the millrace and turned the spring into it. Sela had already taken Ina across the inlet. She remembers the taste of water at the quay and knows nothing of the loan.
3. The six lenders accepted flour in instalments. Their seals and the outstanding measures went into the keeper's archive. After a season at the patched mill, he owes each house one last sack. He can give up his claim to the spring. He cannot give away flour that belongs to those families.
4. The ruined harbour has a second basin with a narrow mouth to the sea. At high tide it fills. Shut its gate, wait for the sea to fall, then release the stored water past a wheel. The city once used it to float laden boats off the mud. Nobody has yet tried it as a millpond.

## Campaign milestones

The levels mark when the existing purchases open. A character can ask for something before a purchase. Its result belongs to confirmed placement. The ten intervening purchases each have their own town exchange. The five chapters use the live order and the same twenty sites.

| Levels | Building | Who needs it and what changes once it stands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–5 | Spring well | Oren tastes the first bucket. Ina stays. Sela promises the people coming off the next boat that they too will be able to sleep by their work. |
| 6–10 | Trapper's cabin | Sela takes Ina to sleep at the trapper's cabin while their roof is open. At dusk she helps the trapper carry empty jars uphill for the morning draw. Her promise will cost somebody that walk every day. |
| 11–15 | Carpenter's workshop | Sela asks for a wooden former and rolls clay around it. Her first pipe fits a broken collar from the quay. She can mend the line if she finds its head. |
| 16–20 | Expedition camp | The pipe crew beds down along the buried line. At supper Sela can offer Oren her bedroll or take his turn carrying water. Either answer earns an invitation to follow the last joint to the headworks. The spring is running into his millrace. |
| 21–25 | Jungle market | Tavi brings water to sell. Sela asks for a place for Ina on the ferry. Tavi hands her the empties to carry back. She promises Ina a turn at the oars the first morning the ferry is free. Six different household seals hang from the flour sacks on her boat. |
| 26–30 | Stone trading post | Sela has the masons dress a fork stone for the spring. She thinks the mill and the quay can each have half. Oren agrees to one trial while he still has meal in store. |
| 31–35 | Water mill | With the repaired wheel and both channels open, grain comes out cracked. Oren makes Sela finish the batch by hand. She closes her own branch before a second sack is spoiled. |
| 36–40 | Tide clock tower | Tavi has the falling water marked against the tower staff. Its lowest mark sits below the sill of the disused harbour basin. Oren agrees to see whether the sea can turn his wheel. |
| 41–45 | Harbour plaza | Sela lays out the basin in chalk in the sun mosaic court. Ferry crews show her where loaded hulls used to rise off the mud. One crew offers to clear the basin mouth if the city will keep it open to boats. |
| 46–50 | Harbour lodge | The first volunteer watch sleeps through the turn. Tavi hangs her ferry bell at the lodge and divides a night between crews. The next watch catches water standing inside the basin after the sea outside has fallen. |
| 51–55 | Stone amphitheatre | The six lenders take seats at a public hearing. Sela asks them to forgive Oren's debt. One woman lent her last jar of seed. She needs her flour as badly as the miller needs his spring. Sela withdraws the request. |
| 56–60 | Cartographer's house | Tavi lays her ferry soundings over the masons' plan. A short cut could carry salt water from the basin to the wheel and back to sea, clear of the freshwater pipes. Oren asks to see whether his mother's pledge names the spring. |
| 61–65 | Keeper's archive | The sealed tally gives the six families flour from the wheel, whatever turns it. Sela can restore the spring to the quay if the tide mill delivers a full grinding. Oren puts his own sack last in the trial. |
| 66–70 | Lighthouse | Tavi agrees on a fire signal with the ferrymen. A boat outside the basin can see when the gate is about to close. For the first time the watch banks a full tide without shutting a loaded boat inside. |
| 71–75 | Beached schooner | The shipwrights draw sound planks from the stranded hull for a sluice leaf. Its weight pulls the test frame sideways. Oren lends the bronze heel from his own mill door for the pivot. |
| 76–80 | Star observatory | The night watchers compare Tavi's soundings across a moon. Between the bigger tides the wheel will have fewer working hours. Oren offers a second milling shift and asks Sela to sit it with him. |
| 81–85 | Great aviary | Tavi sends a homing bird from the mill to its loft with the first shift's tally. The quay can send the next grain boat on the following tide, sparing Oren a night waiting with an empty hopper. |
| 86–90 | Relic museum | Families lend their old grain measures for a public comparison. The seed lender brings the bowl she used last winter. Its chipped rim holds less now. Oren measures her share with the full-sized town bowl. The tide wheel grinds the six final sacks. |
| 91–95 | Temple of the Sun | Before the six lenders, Oren gives up the mill's claim to the spring. Sela beds the new fork stone. The potters and millers each agree to keep a spare gate pin for the other. |
| 96–100 | Jade fountain | Fresh water reaches the lower basin. Sela fires the first water jars in her mother's kiln and hangs her mother's chipped cup on a peg where children can reach it. At the first baking after the trial, Oren comes down to the quay for breakfast. |

## Chapter endings

Each group of twenty levels closes on a decision. At 20, Sela goes to the mill with her brother. At 40, Oren lends his wheel for a tidal trial. At 60, the siblings have a route for the water and go to the archive to find out what they owe. At 80, Sela takes the lean tide’s night shift with Oren. At 100, the player sees what an ordinary morning costs this family now.

## Scenes for the decision

**After restoration 16 · Expedition camp**

- **Oren:** Leave that joint be, Sela. The next one feeds my wheel.
- **Sela:** You knew where the spring went when you lent me the spade?
- **Oren:** I thought you'd find a leak. Come up and see whose grain is in the hopper.

**After restoration 21 · Jungle market**

- **Sela:** Could you bring Ina home on the ferry? She's been on the bank waving an oar at you.
- **Tavi:** She wants my seat. Tell her she can have it the first morning I get off jar duty.
- **Sela:** Leave the empties. I'll carry them up.
- **Tavi:** Take two at a time. That's how I still have a back.

**Level 34 · The trial grinding** *(after the mill stands)*

- **Sela:** Did you get grit in the meal?
- **Oren:** Bite one of the hard bits. It's wheat. Help me grind the rest of it.
- **Sela:** I'll shut my channel. Save me a turn at the stone.

**After restoration 61 · Keeper's archive**

- **Oren:** I carried those sacks. Mother did the talking. What did she put her thumb to?
- **Sela:** “Flour from the mill until each house has its grain back.” She pledged the flour, Oren.
- **Oren:** One sack each and we're square. Will you take the night shift with me?
- **Sela:** Wake me when the tide turns.

**After restoration 91 · Temple of the Sun**

- **Oren:** There's the last sack. Where do you want this stone?
- **Sela:** At the head of the quay pipe. Will you help me set it?
- **Oren:** After the night I've had? Give me the light end.

**Level 100 · Breakfast at the quay**

- **Oren:** Your girl gave me a drink, then asked me for half my breakfast.
- **Sela:** And you gave it to her?
- **Oren:** A crust each way. Tavi's letting her row me home.
