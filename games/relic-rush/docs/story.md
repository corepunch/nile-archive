# Relic Rush · A Crust Each Way

Sela brings her daughter Ina back to an ancient island port after a flood. Her brother Oren mills the town’s grain. Tavi runs the ferry. The first bucket lets one family stay. To bring the other families home, the player must get fresh water down to the quay.

## Truth timeline

1. Last winter the sea broke the quay pipes and the mill dam. Six households lent seed grain to feed the refugees. Oren’s mother pledged flour in return. Oren put the hillside spring through his wheel.
2. Sela follows the buried pipe to the mill. Dividing the spring leaves too little water to grind. Oren still owes each lender a sack.
3. Tavi measures the drop between a full boat basin and the ebbing sea. A separate saltwater channel can drive the wheel without taking drinking water.
4. The tide wheel grinds the six sacks. Oren releases the spring. Ina rows him across for breakfast.

## Plants and payoffs

| First detail | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| Ina comes home with a single cup. | It hangs at the fountain within reach of the next child. |
| Six different seals hang from Oren’s sacks. | The seal impressions in the archive identify the lenders and what they are owed. |
| A ferry stays afloat inside the basin at low tide. | The head of trapped water runs the millwheel. |
| Tavi promises Ina the oars when she finishes hauling water. | Oren pays Ina a crust for each crossing. |
