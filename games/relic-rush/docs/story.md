# Story: three episodes to "To be continued"

**Status: implemented in the playable thirty-level campaign.** All 214 lines play, with the four existing authored portraits and Kessler's portrait-less radio captions; story scenes play over the existing island and scene paintings. The structure follows the proposal in [the research report](../../../reports/Прогрессия%20June%20Journey%20и%20Tropicats.md): 30 levels, wonders split 4+3+3, one beat per level, a hook at each episode's end. Plot, characters and names are our own; only the pacing comes from the references.

No new busts are needed. All dialogue uses the four existing busts (Elena, Mara, Tomas, Adrian), plus Kessler, who is only ever heard. All 10 wonders are the existing ones, in their existing order.

**Feeling:** a pulp adventure serial. Traps that go off, a runaway cart, a rival you only ever hear, and a cliffhanger at the end of every episode. The danger is real but nobody dies on screen, and the jokes keep coming while the floor moves.

Techniques only, recalled from the films themselves. No plot, line, character or set piece is copied.

## The question

**Who silenced the temple, and where did everyone go?** Elena asks it in level 3 and level 7. Episode 1 shows that someone did it, episode 2 names who, and episode 3 explains why. Over it all hangs a race: **who reaches the Heart first, Elena or Kessler?** The ending answers both and replaces them with a bigger one: what is out there?

## What really happened

The player learns this in pieces. Writers need it whole.

1. **Long ago.** The Heart of the Sun, set in the temple, warms the deep spring and makes the sunstones sing at noon across the whole valley. One day a light answers from the sea. The valley people pack, latch their doors and sail towards it. The temple keeps singing to an empty valley for centuries.
2. **Thirty years ago.** Cornelius Vale arrives with eleven men, among them young Tomas Reed. They find the singing temple and see the light. Cornelius takes a boat and ten men to follow it. His orders to Tomas: silence it until I come back, or the whole world will come. He writes a letter for his five-year-old son, Adrian, which Tomas posts on the last supply boat.
3. **The thirty years since.** Tomas cuts out the Heart with Cornelius's chisel and hides it under the old fountain, where it is the only warmth left. Without it, frost climbs from the deep halls. Sunstones crack from the inside. Tomas scratches his own name off the wall, tears the last log pages out, measures the frost every morning and waits.
4. **Now.** Adrian has borrowed against the Heart from the Meridian Society to fund Elena's dig and find his father. Their man, Kessler, follows on the *Halcyon*. Adrian pockets a split sunstone on the first day. On the last night he opens the letter, and when he has to choose he hands Kessler the sunstone in his jacket and brings the Heart home.

## Cast arcs

| Character | Starts as | Ends as | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Elena Voss** | Careful scholar who follows evidence and wants nothing removed | Leader who puts the Heart back knowing the risk, and sets off after the lights | Dry, exact. Her wrong theories are honest and she says so when they fall |
| **Mara Bell** | Pilot and comic relief | The one who reads the bearings and spots what others miss, and the expedition's way forward | Jokes first, then the useful fact. Her plane is the *Marigold*. Names every snake |
| **Tomas Reed** | Evasive hermit archivist | The man who silenced the temple out of loyalty, and regrets it | Formal, courteous, answers a different question from the one asked |
| **Adrian Vale** | Charming financier, likely villain | A son who cons his creditors, gives the Heart back and goes looking for his father | Light and self-mocking, most when he is most afraid |
| **Kessler** | A polite voice on the radio | Still a voice, now sailing after the lights with a stone in a jacket | Unfailingly courteous, which is the frightening part. Never raises his voice |

Kessler has no bust on purpose: he is only ever heard, over the *Marigold*'s radio or a loudhailer from his launch. The engine shows his lines as a caption with his name, and his face is saved for a sequel.

**Gameplay ties.** Ice is the frost story: it arrives as a blocker in level 6, the same level the frost is first noticed, and belongs to the deep halls. Sand and chains stay as they are, digging and vines. Level 8 is renamed from *Prism Observatory*, because the observatory is not built until level 23.

## How beats play

Order: result → island → place the wonder if one was earned → beat → next goal. A level without a wonder plays its beat on returning to the island. Every beat plays once per save, on the first clear only. Replays show nothing.

**Wonders keep their `about` line** in the bubble over the building, so Tomas no longer has to read it out. That also lets others speak at wonders while Tomas is missing (levels 23 and 29).

Bubble kinds: lines are speech unless marked *(thought)* or *(shout)*.

## Episode 1: The Singing Gate

**Before level 1 · opening** · *Panel: the Marigold skidding onto black sand under a cliff; in the cliff, a carved gate standing half open.*

- **Mara (shout):** Hold on to something! The Marigold doesn't like this beach, and the feeling's mutual!

- **Elena:** Look at the arch. Every stone is cut to ring. The whole gate is an instrument.
- **Mara:** It's ticking, is what it is. Elena, your foot's on a loose one.
- **Elena:** Oh. Oh, that's a trigger. Mara, run!
- **Mara (shout):** Under! Get under it! Now!
- **Adrian:** Well. That was the door. I assume there's another way out.
- **Elena:** You hired me to record this place, Adrian. Nothing leaves the valley. That was our deal.
- **Adrian:** Of course. I've a letter here, to be opened when we find the Heart of the Sun. Pure sentiment.
- **Mara:** Who carries a sealed letter into a booby trap?
- **Adrian:** A man with excellent manners.

**Level 1 · First Light · builds Expedition camp**

- **Elena:** I'm Dr Voss, and this island isn't on any chart. Who are you?

- **Tomas:** Some doors close for a reason, Miss Bell.

**Level 2 · The Jade Crescent**

- **Elena:** These sunstones didn't break with age. They split from the inside, every edge clean.
- **Mara:** Snake. Big one. On your boot. Don't panic, I'm calling him Gerald.

**Level 3 · Sunstone Spiral**

**Level 4 · The Vine Constellation · builds Spring well**

## Episode 2: The Vale Expedition

**Before level 11 · opening** · *Panel: the same chamber, moments later. Adrian on the steps below the gold disc, turning the envelope over; Elena and Mara watching.*

- **Adrian:** Cornelius Vale, my father. He came here thirty years ago with eleven men and a great many crates.
- **Adrian:** None of them came home. I was five. All that ever reached me was this envelope, through a lawyer.
- **Elena:** So you lied to get me here.
- **Adrian:** You'd never have come for a missing man. You came for a lost valley.
- **Mara:** And Kessler? Where does he fit?
- **Adrian:** I borrowed against the Heart to pay for all this. If we find it, it's theirs. If we don't, so am I.
- **Mara:** Open the letter, then.
- **Adrian:** "When the Heart is found." It isn't found, is it? It's the one thing he ever asked of me.
- **Elena:** Then we look for your father too. And you stop deciding what I get to know.
- **Adrian:** That sounds entirely fair. I shall try to be bad at it.

**Level 11 · The Long Terrace**

- **Elena:** End of the line. His dig site. Your father hauled something heavy up here, Adrian.
- **Adrian:** He built everything as though he'd be back on Monday.

**Level 12 · Twelve Crates**

- **Mara:** Nobody walks away from a year's tinned peaches.
- **Adrian:** Careful with that lid, there's a wire. Ah. A spring trap. Father always did hate thieves.

**Level 13 · Names in the Stone · builds Sunstone courtyard**

- **Mara:** That's eleven. Twelve went in, counting him.
- **Elena:** The twelfth has been cut away. Carefully, by someone who took his time.
- **Mara:** Who chisels a man off a wall and then tidies up the dust?

**Level 14 · Static**

- **Mara:** Caught our friend on the radio at two in the morning. Guess who.
- **Kessler:** You're very quiet tonight, Mr Vale. I do hope you haven't found something without me.
- **Adrian:** Not a thing. A gold disc with a hole in it. You'd be bored stiff.
- **Elena:** You told him about the disc?
- **Adrian:** I told him it was empty. A lie that sounds like bad news buys time. It's a family skill.

**Level 15 · The Frost Line**

- **Elena:** The frost is over the second terrace. Tomas said a little every year.
- **Mara:** That's not a little. That's ten feet since Sunday, and it's speeding up.
- **Elena:** And it's spreading out from the temple, as if whatever warmed this valley was taken out of it.
- **Elena:** But look at my map. One patch the frost won't touch, right in the middle of the valley.
- **Mara:** So whoever cut the Heart out never took it off the island. It's hidden here, still warm.

**Level 16 · Supper for Twelve · builds Explorer's lodge**

- **Mara:** Alive for thirty years on what, tinned peaches?

**Level 17 · The Torn Log**

## Episode 3: The Heart of the Sun
