# Relic Rush: A Light Below

**Status:** Fresh campaign plan and playable first chapter. Chapters 2–3 have provisional bridge dialogue so existing level slots remain coherent; they need a full dialogue pass and cold read. The town tracks quests and decoration points. Chapter 1 uses the existing character portraits and island views.

**Play fantasy:** Rebuild an expedition base and follow a light answering from beneath the island. The feeling is curious, warm-hearted pulp adventure: danger gives friends a reason to act for one another.

## What is broken

The camp is abandoned, the well is fouled, old paths are overgrown, and an unattended tide relay sends a false all-clear. Its lamp is useful to boats but can draw them towards a damaged quay. The existing sand, vines, chains and ice are obstacles, not evidence by themselves. Restorations give the team places to work and meet.

## Truth timeline

1. Years ago island keeper Ines maintained a tide-room relay to warn boats away from the lower quay when its landing was unsafe. The chart behind the well pump records the relay route; the lamp bracket at camp lets a lookout answer.
2. Tomas and Ines worked together. When the quay wall cracked, Tomas closed the room; Ines left to fetch repair plans and supplies. Tomas promised to maintain the warning, but a storm cut the cable. He believes Ines did not return.
3. Recently Ines returned by a different shore. She found the room flooded and, unable to reach camp, used its hand shutter to send the old warning. She changed its rhythm to attract attention. Tomas does not know she is back.
4. Adrian financed the expedition using a promised exclusive claim on island discoveries. He has not told Elena; the claim puts the relay and islanders' work at risk. Kessler represents the claim holder, not a supernatural threat.
5. The team repairs the route and relay, finds Ines's signed repair note and learns the warning's purpose. Tomas chooses to share his old responsibility rather than hide his mistake. Adrian gives up the claim, and the crew makes the quay safe. Ines leaves a chart to other warning stations; their keepers may need help too.

## Question and cast

Mara asks in the opening: **Who is signalling back?** The first chapter answers that someone is using the old relay and asks who returned. The second identifies Ines and shows why Tomas feared the answer. The third asks whether the crew can keep the quay safe without surrendering it.

The wrong theory is Elena's automatic reflection at 3. The changed rhythm breaks it at 10, rather than identifying its sender without evidence. Film technique: *Raiders of the Lost Ark* uses a practical object as both tool and clue; here Mara's lamp has both jobs. No plot or dialogue is borrowed.

## Delivery and choices

On boards 1–10 the named quest object is the win condition. The same object appears in the goal and in the first-clear exchange. Each first clear plays a short exchange before the reward card. The tide-room reveal plays after Claim and leads into a choice of who answers the signal. Confirmed placements play their own scene. Replays earn Amulets. On reload, the next unread scene or selected reply resumes, while completed boards and placed buildings stay credited. Older saves receive a recap at their current progress.
