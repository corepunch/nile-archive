# Story: three episodes to "To be continued"

**Status: implemented in the playable thirty-level campaign.** All 183 lines use the four existing authored portraits; story scenes play over the existing island and scene paintings. The structure follows the proposal in [the research report](../../../reports/Прогрессия%20June%20Journey%20и%20Tropicats.md): 30 levels, wonders split 4+3+3, one beat per level, a hook at each episode's end. Plot, characters and names are our own; only the pacing comes from the references.

No new speakers are needed. All dialogue uses the four existing busts (Elena, Mara, Tomas, Adrian) and all 10 wonders are the existing ones, in their existing order.

## The question

**Who silenced the temple, and where did everyone go?** Elena asks it in level 3 and level 5. Episode 1 shows that someone did it, episode 2 names who, and episode 3 explains why. The ending answers both halves and replaces them with a bigger one: what is out there?

## What really happened

The player learns this in pieces. Writers need it whole.

1. **Long ago.** The Heart of the Sun, set in the temple, warms the deep spring and makes the sunstones sing at noon across the whole valley. One day a light answers from the sea. The valley people pack, latch their doors and sail towards it. The temple keeps singing to an empty valley for centuries.
2. **Thirty years ago.** Cornelius Vale arrives with eleven men, among them young Tomas Reed. They find the singing temple and see the light. Cornelius takes a boat and ten men to follow it. His orders to Tomas: silence it until I come back, or the whole world will come. He writes a letter for his five-year-old son, Adrian, which Tomas posts on the last supply boat.
3. **The thirty years since.** Tomas cuts out the Heart with Cornelius's chisel and hides it under the old fountain, where it is the only warmth left. Without it, frost climbs from the deep halls. Sunstones crack from the inside. Tomas scratches his own name off the wall, tears the last log pages out, measures the frost every morning and waits.
4. **Now.** Adrian, in debt to the Meridian Society, funds Elena's dig to find his father, and hides that from her.

**Gameplay ties.** Ice is the frost story: it arrives as a blocker in level 6, the same level the frost is first noticed, and belongs to the deep halls. Sand and chains stay as they are, digging and vines. Level 8 is renamed from *Prism Observatory*, because the observatory is not built until level 23.

## How beats play

Order: result → island → place the wonder if one was earned → beat → next goal. A level without a wonder plays its beat on returning to the island. Every beat plays once per save, on the first clear only. Replays show nothing.

**Wonders keep their `about` line** in the bubble over the building, so Tomas no longer has to read it out. That also lets others speak at wonders while Tomas is missing (levels 23 and 26).

Bubble kinds: lines are speech unless marked *(thought)* or *(shout)*.

## Episode 1: The Silent Valley

**Before level 1 · opening** · *Panel: the Marigold over the island's green crater at dawn.*

- **Elena (thought):** The Lost Valley at last. Broken sunstones, silent terraces... someone lived here once.
- **Mara:** And now we do. I'll keep the Marigold ready. You find out who they were.
- **Adrian:** Do find something my sponsors can admire. They paid for rather a lot of canvas.
- **Elena:** We're here to record this place, Adrian. Nothing leaves the valley. That's the deal.
- **Adrian:** Of course. I've a letter here, to be opened when we find the Heart of the Sun. Pure sentiment.
- **Mara:** Who carries a sealed letter into a jungle?
- **Adrian:** A man with excellent manners.
- **Elena:** Every puzzle is an excavation. Let's start digging.

**Level 1 · First Light · builds Expedition camp**

- **Tomas:** You'll want that camp further from the cliff. It crumbles after the rains.
- **Elena:** I'm Dr Voss, and this island isn't on any chart. Who are you?
- **Tomas:** Tomas Reed. I look after the archive. What's left of it.
- **Mara:** A librarian in the jungle. I've had stranger Tuesdays, but not many.

**Level 2 · The Jade Crescent**

- **Elena:** These sunstones didn't break with age. Look at the edges.
- **Elena:** No hammer marks either. They split from the inside, every one.
- **Mara:** Stone doesn't just do that, does it?
- **Tomas:** Stone does many things, given time.

**Level 3 · Sunstone Spiral**

- **Elena:** Strange. Strike this stone and it hums. They're all tuned.
- **Tomas:** They sang once. Every noon, the whole valley, from the temple down.
- **Elena:** You make it sound as if you'd heard it.
- **Tomas:** It's in the records.
- **Elena (thought):** A temple that sings doesn't simply stop. Someone silenced it.

**Level 4 · The Vine Constellation · builds Spring well**

- **Mara:** Good water. And a lovely brass pump, from entirely the wrong century.
- **Elena:** Threaded fittings. Someone was here before us, and not so long ago.
- **Adrian:** Impossible. No expedition has ever been recorded here. I checked very carefully.
- **Mara:** Funny thing to check so carefully.
- **Tomas:** The spring runs lower every year. I'd drink while you can.

**Level 5 · Three Paths Meet**

- **Mara:** Took the Marigold up. All three valley roads run down to the same beach.
- **Elena:** Any graves? Burned houses? Anything?
- **Mara:** Not a one. A whole people walked to the water and stopped existing.
- **Elena:** Two questions, then. Who silenced the temple? And where did everyone go?

**Level 6 · Winter Lotus**

- **Elena:** Mara, touch this wall. Is that... frost?
- **Mara:** In this heat? I'm sweating through my good jacket.
- **Tomas:** The lower halls are cold now. It climbs a little higher every year.
- **Elena:** Climbs from where?
- **Tomas:** Mind your footing down there, Dr Voss.

**Level 7 · The Ember Ring · builds Artisan workshop**

- **Elena:** Raiders. It has to be. They smashed the stones and drove everyone out.
- **Adrian:** A tidy theory. Raiders, however, rarely sweep up.
- **Elena:** ...Every tool on its hook. Every door latched. And frost split those stones, not raiders.
- **Mara:** Nobody robbed these people. They packed.
- **Elena:** I was wrong, then. So what makes a whole valley leave on purpose?

**Level 8 · Prism Hollow** *(easier level)*

- **Tomas:** The frost passed the first terrace last night. I'd not planned on it so soon.
- **Elena:** Planned on it? Tomas, what do you know?
- **Tomas:** That you should dig faster.

**Level 9 · The Sixfold Seal · builds Jungle market**

- **Mara:** Best stall in the market, and look what's under the floor. Six-sided stonework.
- **Elena:** Six sides, six locks. It's a seal, and it runs right under the temple terrace.
- **Adrian:** Then the Heart of the Sun is beneath it. How marvellous.
- **Elena:** You sound very sure.
- **Adrian:** I'm an optimist. It's very nearly a profession.

**Before level 10**

- **Elena:** Last seal. Whatever stopped the song is under here.
- **Mara:** Or nothing is, and we've dug up a very fancy floor.
- **Tomas:** Go carefully. Some things are quiet for a reason.

**Level 10 · Heart of the Hexagon · hook** · *Panel: lamplight on a great golden sun disc with a raw hole at its centre.*

- **Elena:** A sun of solid gold, wide as a cartwheel. And cold as a well.
- **Adrian:** The Heart of the Sun. Elena, we've actually...
- **Elena:** Look closer. That's a socket. The Heart was here, and someone cut it out.
- **Mara:** Who steals the heart out of a temple?
- **Elena:** Someone with good tools. They left one behind. A steel chisel, thirty years old at most.
- **Mara:** Any maker's mark?
- **Elena:** Vale and Sons.
- **Tomas (thought):** So. He sent his boy.
- **Elena:** Adrian. Why is your name on this?
- **Adrian:** Ah.
- **Adrian:** You'd better sit down, Elena. That chisel was my father's.

## Episode 2: The Vale Expedition

**Before level 11 · opening** · *Panel: the same chamber, moments later. Adrian on the steps below the gold disc, turning the envelope over; Elena and Mara watching.*

- **Adrian:** Cornelius Vale, my father. He came here thirty years ago with eleven men and a great many crates.
- **Adrian:** None of them came home. I was five. All that ever reached me was this envelope, through a lawyer.
- **Elena:** So you lied to get me here.
- **Adrian:** You'd never have come for a missing man. You came for a lost valley.
- **Mara:** Open the letter.
- **Adrian:** "When the Heart is found." It isn't found, is it? It's the one thing he ever asked of me.
- **Elena:** Then we look for your father too. And you stop deciding what I get to know.
- **Adrian:** That sounds entirely fair. I shall try to be bad at it.

**Level 11 · The Long Terrace**

- **Mara:** Follow the rust. Narrow rail, right across the terrace. Lovely work, terrible manners.
- **Elena:** They hauled something heavy uphill and never hauled it back down.
- **Adrian:** He built everything like that. As though he'd be back on Monday.
- **Tomas:** Rails rust quickly here. I'd not read too much into it.

**Level 12 · Twelve Crates**

- **Elena:** Supply crates. Food for twelve men for a year, and most of it never opened.
- **Mara:** Nobody walks away from a year's tinned peaches.
- **Adrian:** He left in a hurry, then. He never hurried in his life.

**Level 13 · Names in the Stone · builds Sunstone courtyard**

- **Elena:** Names cut into the courtyard wall. A fine old expedition habit.
- **Adrian:** Cornelius Vale, first. Naturally. Then ten more I can read.
- **Mara:** That's eleven. Twelve went in, counting him.
- **Elena:** The twelfth has been cut away. Carefully, by someone who took his time.
- **Mara:** Who chisels a man off a wall and then tidies up the dust?

**Level 14 · Static**

- **Mara:** A word, Elena. Our friend's been on my radio at night. Reports to the Meridian Society.
- **Elena:** Adrian? What did you tell them?
- **Adrian:** That we're close. They hold the debt on this whole expedition, Elena. And all of mine.
- **Mara:** They also hold a ship. The Halcyon sails the day the Heart turns up.

**Level 15 · The Frost Line**

- **Elena:** The frost is over the second terrace. Tomas said a little every year.
- **Mara:** That's not a little. That's ten feet since Sunday.
- **Elena:** It's speeding up. When it reaches the well, this valley has no water.

**Level 16 · Supper for Twelve · builds Explorer's lodge**

- **Mara:** Twelve bunks. Plates still on the table. They got up in the middle of supper.
- **Elena:** Eleven bunks under thirty years of dust. And one with a clean blanket.
- **Adrian:** One of them stayed. Someone alive can tell me what happened.
- **Mara:** Alive for thirty years on what, tinned peaches?
- **Elena:** Whoever it is made that bed this morning.

**Level 17 · The Torn Log**

- **Elena:** It fits. Your father cut out the Heart, crated it and sailed. That's the whole story.
- **Adrian:** Then why is his best crate still up here, nailed shut and empty?
- **Elena:** Then I was wrong to call it the whole story. We haven't proved the Heart ever left.
- **Mara:** And here's their log. The last pages are torn out.
- **Elena:** By whoever owns that clean blanket.

**Level 18 · Lantern Steps** *(easier level)*

- **Tomas:** Stop digging, Dr Voss. Take your plane and your financier and go home. Please.
- **Elena:** The well has weeks left. You know that better than anyone.
- **Tomas:** I know exactly what's left. I've measured it every morning for thirty years.

**Level 19 · Under the Vines · builds Lost-world archive**

- **Elena:** He's kept every page in here dry. All but the ones we need.
- **Mara:** Found a photograph in the back of a ledger. Twelve men on a beach.
- **Adrian:** That's my father. And beside him, the thin lad in spectacles...
- **Elena:** Add a grey beard. That's Tomas.
- **Mara:** Our twelfth name. Our librarian.
- **Elena:** He told me the song was in the records. It isn't. He heard it.

**Before level 20**

- **Elena:** He's gone down to the seal. He left a lamp burning so we'd follow.
- **Adrian:** Let me speak to him first.
- **Mara:** Not a chance. You'd offer him a loan.

**Level 20 · The Archivist's Key · hook** · *Panel: Tomas by the empty socket, one lamp, three shadows reaching for him.*

- **Elena:** You took the Heart. You silenced the temple.
- **Tomas:** I did. With Cornelius's own chisel, the morning he left. I would do it again.
- **Elena:** Your silence is killing the spring. The frost that split those stones is your doing.
- **Tomas:** I know what it costs. I have watched it climb for thirty years.
- **Adrian:** Where is my father? Did you kill him?
- **Tomas:** Kill him? He made me swear. "Keep it silent, Tomas, until I come back."
- **Adrian:** Come back from where?
- **Tomas:** Ask where an entire people went, Mr Vale. Ask what they heard.
- **Elena:** Tomas. Where is the Heart?
- **Tomas:** Where the cold can't reach it. Go home, all of you.
- **Mara (shout):** Elena, the lamp! He's put it out. He's gone!

## Episode 3: The Heart of the Sun

**Before level 21 · opening** · *Panel: the Marigold's cockpit, Tomas's journal open on the seat, a ship's smoke far off through the windscreen.*

- **Mara:** He left this on my pilot's seat. His journal. No note, just one page marked.
- **Elena:** A star chart with six points ringed. It would only read properly from an observatory.
- **Adrian:** He wants to be found.
- **Elena:** Or he wants the Heart found before the well runs dry, and can't bring himself to say so.
- **Mara:** Also, the radio says the Halcyon's sailed. Six days out.
- **Adrian:** I didn't tell them. I stopped answering. Evidently that told them enough.
- **Elena:** Then we need that observatory before either of them reaches us.
- **Mara:** Frost, a ship and a runaway librarian. Honestly? Best job I've ever had.

**Level 21 · Tomas's Trail**

- **Elena:** His boot prints climb through the frost, then stop dead at running water.
- **Mara:** Thirty years on one island. Of course he knows how to vanish.
- **Adrian:** All my life I've wanted someone to blame. And all he ever did was wait.

**Level 22 · The Frozen Stair**

- **Adrian:** I've never held a pick in my life. I believe I've broken it.
- **Elena:** That's a shovel, Adrian.
- **Adrian:** Then I've broken that too. Send the bill to the Meridian Society.

**Level 23 · The Star Road · builds Star observatory**

- **Elena:** The chart fits the dome. Six points, and none of them where a star should be.
- **Mara:** Give it here. Those aren't stars, they're bearings. It's how I'd mark headings at sea.
- **Elena:** Six places, then. And one of them is us.
- **Adrian:** Why would anyone mark out five other islands?
- **Elena:** There's a note beside ours. "Noon shadow, the old spring."

**Level 24 · Noon Shadow**

- **Elena:** At noon the temple's shadow falls right across the old fountain.
- **Mara:** So the librarian's hiding under a fountain. In a valley running out of water.
- **Elena:** In the one place the frost hasn't touched. We have to get that fountain running.

**Level 25 · Smoke on the Water**

- **Mara:** Smoke on the horizon. Two days, less if the wind holds.
- **Adrian:** They're asking for me on the radio. Very politely. That's the worst kind.
- **Elena:** Will you answer?
- **Adrian:** I haven't decided what I am yet, Elena. Ask me tomorrow.

**Level 26 · The Jade Deep · builds Jade fountain**

- **Elena:** The basin's draining into a stair. And the air coming up is warm.
- **Tomas:** You took your time. The only warm room left in the valley, and I've kept it for you.
- **Mara:** Elena, look at his coat. It's glowing right through the cloth.
- **Tomas:** The sea answered the Heart's song. A light on the water. The valley followed it centuries ago.
- **Tomas:** Cornelius saw it too. "Silence it until I'm back, or the whole world will come."
- **Elena:** And he left you here to watch it freeze.

**Level 27 · Six Sun Gates**

- **Elena:** Thirty years you've told yourself that silence keeps everyone safe.
- **Tomas:** Did it? Look at the terraces. I kept my word and killed the place he loved.
- **Elena:** So silence isn't safety. It's only a slower way to lose everything.
- **Mara:** So we light it, and whoever's out there learns where we live. Lovely.

**Level 28 · The Last Terrace** *(easier level)*

- **Mara (shout):** Elena! The Heart's gone, and there's a dinghy missing from the beach!
- **Elena:** Adrian, stop! That's the only warmth this valley has left!
- **Adrian:** It buys back every debt I have, Elena. There's nothing else I own that could.
- **Elena:** Then open your letter. The Heart is found. Those were the terms.
- **Adrian:** ..."My boy. Put it back. Then come and find me. C.V."

**Level 29 · The Silent Temple · builds Temple of the sun**

- **Adrian:** The Heart of the Sun... remarkable. My sponsors would have been delighted.
- **Elena:** It stays in the valley, Adrian. That was always the deal.
- **Adrian:** So it was. Take it, before I remember what I owe.
- **Tomas:** He sealed that letter the morning he sailed. I never read it. I only posted it.
- **Elena:** The temple's whole again. There's only one thing left to do.

**Before level 30**

- **Mara:** The Halcyon's anchored in the bay. They'll be ashore by noon.
- **Elena:** Then we finish by noon.
- **Tomas:** Noon is when it sings.

**Level 30 · The Song Returns · hook** · *Panel: the temple blazing, the whole valley lit, and far out at sea a thin column of light.*

- **Elena:** Set it in. Gently... there.
- **Tomas:** Listen.
- **Mara:** Is that all of it? The whole valley?
- **Elena:** The frost's running off the terraces. The well is filling. Tomas, it's singing.
- **Tomas:** Thirty years. I'm sorry, Cornelius.
- **Adrian:** Elena. You'd better look at the sea.
- **Mara (shout):** There! A light on the water! And past it, another one!
- **Elena:** Five of them. Five bearings, just as he marked. Something out there is answering.
- **Tomas:** That's where they went. All of them.
- **Adrian:** And my father.
- **Mara:** Worse news. The Halcyon's weighing anchor. She's not landing, she's heading for the lights.
- **Elena:** Then fill the Marigold's tanks, Mara. We have a race to win.

- Stable `narrative.v1.<phase>-<level>` keys are persisted after completion/Skip. An unfinished beat remains unacknowledged and restarts after reload; replays and retries are silent.
- Version 3 saves retain level counts, purchases, wallet and open regions. A short recap bridges the old dialogue. Existing later wonders count as already restored when their story milestone arrives.
- The scene-panel notes above describe composition and events. The implemented presentation intentionally uses the existing paintings and four authored busts; separate cinematic illustrations are optional future art direction.
- Dialogue body text is 31.5 points, names and building text 22.5 points: 50% larger. Representative longest-line, thought, shout and finale captures cover both phone sizes.
