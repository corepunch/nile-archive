# Level tuning

Generated by `python3 games/relic-rush/tools/tune_levels.py` from the curve in
[lua/difficulty.lua](../lua/difficulty.lua). A greedy bot (highest-valued legal swap,
no tools) plays hashed-seed starts. Each board keeps its plaque count; the patch is its
authored shape moved up to two cells (shifted) or a compact patch grown around one cell
(grown). Bare win is the chosen patch's win rate at the curve budget with no blockers.
The bot then plays with every board spawning six kinds and refill
damping from the second cascade wave. Its rates order and space the boards; they
are not measured human win rates. Median and fastest count the bot's winning games.
