One wide two-state seamless material swatch sheet for Relic Rush, a tropical archaeological expedition match-3 game. EXACTLY two square, fully painted stone material swatches, side by side occupying the full sheet, very readable at tiny cell sizes. LEFT HALF: warm beige light sandstone, matte and subtle mottling, quiet and even with very fine worn grain. RIGHT HALF: saturated lagoon blue glazed stone, darker than beige by a clear value step, with tangible but understated pale blue geographic veins and a very subtle softly mottled enamel gloss. Each is seen directly overhead with no perspective. A single uninterrupted texture per swatch fills all pixels up to each edge; no external tile lines or bevels, no silhouettes, no letters, no pictograms, no shadows, no lettering, no ornate design, no border. The two swatches use comparable microtexture and make a clear on-board difference even in grayscale and at 40px. These swatches will be cropped at their centers and fitted to an existing flat-top hex mask.
