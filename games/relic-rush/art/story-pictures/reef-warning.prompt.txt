Original square full-bleed story painting for a vintage tropical island adventure. View from a small dinghy just offshore, at blue dusk. Composition makes a sailor's discovery legible at small phone scale: a few black reef teeth breach the rippling water immediately in front of us. Directly above the reef in the distance, a squat weathered stone harbor lookout on an island cliff shows ONE vivid ruby red square lantern in its open viewing port. A delicate narrow red reflection threads the water from that lamp toward the dark reef teeth. The open-water passage to the right is moonlit teal. Tiny strip of worn dinghy bow at the bottom, no people visible. Strong quiet composition, intimate scale, tactile painterly gouache, rich deep teal shadows, coral red signal, old brass and warm stone. Natural beacon, absolutely no laser beam or outlined diagram, no arrows, no letters, no text, no borders. The lamp is on the near harbor lookout, viewed by the skiff; reef directly aligned with lamp and viewer. No lighthouse tower, no modern machinery.
