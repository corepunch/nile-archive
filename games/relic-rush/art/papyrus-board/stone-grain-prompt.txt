Use case: stylized-concept. Create one original tileable square stone texture for the border sprites of a painted archaeology match-3 game. A flat, face-on cut of dry pale sandstone. This is a material map for Multiply blending, so the overall value must be light, with a bone-white to light warm grey ground, moderately contrasted grey-brown mineral freckles and pits, and fine irregular bands of sediment. Give the irregular marks several scales so they remain readable in a 7 px wide frame. Fine weathered stone texture, not cloth, wood or paper. A few soft small flecks, restrained darker pores, matte painterly grain inspired by classic hand-painted game stone. Completely fill every pixel of the square. Seamlessly tileable at all four edges. No frame, no border, no bevel, no cast shadow, no directional lighting, no central object, no leaf, no grid of stones, no mortar lines, no lines that split the image in half, no lettering. One complete opaque square only.
