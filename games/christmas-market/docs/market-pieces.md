# From Rome to Bellmere

Construction introduces the goods the next stalls need. The first purchases
follow the GameTop Windows [Cradle of Rome 2 purchase table](../../../docs/cradle-of-rome-reference/rome-2-economy.json).
Bellmere keeps three wallet slots in save order: **Tokens**, **Treats** and
**Materials**. Bell tokens, cocoa and bundled pine identify the three totals
across the board, town, prices and results.

## Color map

| Cradle of Rome 2 | Bellmere color | Christmas Market piece and meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green leaf (`gmbn_1`) | Spruce green | Holly sprig for score. Its board position later holds the green farrier's hammer. |
| Blue flower (`gmbn_2`) | Royal blue | Knitted mitten for score. Its position becomes yellow when the lumber workshop opens. |
| Silver coin (`gmbn_3`, Gold +3) | Silver white | Round star token, +3 Tokens. |
| Gold coin (`gmbn_4`, Gold +5) | Violet | Six-sided bell token, +5 Tokens. Future high-value ornaments use the same violet position. |
| Fifth starting position | Cranberry red | Candy cane for score. Cider, gingerbread and cocoa replace it in succession. |
| Brown bucket with blue water (`gmbn_5`, Food +3) | Cranberry red | Spiced cider in a tall, fluted tumbler, +3 Treats. |
| Steel and wood hammer (`600`) | Spruce green | Farrier's hammer, +3 charge. |
| Brown logs (`gmbn_6`, Supplies +5) | Warm yellow | Bundled pine with green cord, +5 Materials. |
| Charcoal bomb (`601`) | Evergreen enamel | Firework Bomb in the toolbar, one use per wish. |
| Red meat and golden bread (`gmbn_7`, `gmbn_9`, Food +4 and +5) | Cranberry red | Glazed gingerbread star, then hot cocoa in a round mug with a cream cap. |
| Gray stone (`gmbn_8`, Supplies +7) | Warm yellow | Wooden toy blocks, +7 Materials, reserved for the later Toymaker's workshop. |

Every wish spawns five kinds. The first four wishes use green, blue, silver,
violet and red. Wish five trades blue for yellow. Later red treats and violet
collectibles take turns in their respective positions. The red star still has
a distinct silhouette from the silver token, and the mug and the tumbler are
recognizable even when shown together in a reward or wallet.

## Playable construction sequence

| Purchase | After wish | Rome II counterpart | Bellmere reward | Price (Tokens / Treats / Materials) |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | ---: |
| Welcome arch | 1 | Well | Spiced cider. A hot drink for arrivals from the station. | 50 / 0 / 0 |
| Reindeer stable | 2 | Hunter's Hut | Farrier's hammer. Match 30 to charge a single-cell hit. | 60 / 20 / 0 |
| Lumber workshop | 4 | Sawmill | Bundled pine. Theo's cut wood pays for the market's next stalls. | 100 / 75 / 0 |
| Gingerbread house | 5 | Village | One Firework Bomb per wish for a 3×3 burst. | 150 / 40 / 80 |
| Gingerbread bakery | 7 | Farm | Glazed gingerbread stars take over the Treats position at +4. | 300 / 0 / 200 |
| Hot cocoa kiosk | 9 | Mine slot, with the Mill's +5 Food role | The kiosk's cocoa takes over the Treats position at +5. The Toymaker's workshop later takes the Mine's +7 Materials role. | 400 / 100 / 300 |

The reward starts after confirmed placement and applies from the following
wish. Replays use the goods available to that wish. In the extended campaign
test, the greedy player won all ten wishes and funded all six purchases at their
gates. The sample took four retries after losses and zero replays for income.
