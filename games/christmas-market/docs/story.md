# Story: a market by Christmas Eve

**Status: simple scenes.** One opening, a short scene when each of the twenty stalls is placed, and one every five wishes or so. It follows the [story guide](../../../docs/STORY_WRITING.md). The plot, cast and setting are original. The longer scripted campaign this replaces (four threads, Kind Word notes, resident visits) is in git history before 2026-11-23.

**Fantasy of play:** I came to help Bellmere get its Christmas market going again. Every stall I put up, somebody moves in and gets on with Christmas.

**Feeling:** a snowy December in a small town. People sell trees, bake, wrap, sing flat and wait for trains. Nobody needs anything from the player but company. There's no villain, no secret and no task list. The one goal is said at the start: the market should be open by Christmas Eve, and on Christmas Eve it is.

## How scenes play

- **Opening.** Before the first board, Noelle and Agnes welcome the player to Bellmere.
- **New stalls.** Placing a stall plays its scene once, after the player confirms the site. Someone starts using it: Theo sets out his firs under the arch, Pip feeds the reindeer. A scene talks only about its own stall.
- **Along the way.** Every five wishes (3, 8, 13 … 98) and on the hundredth, a first clear plays a short scene before the result card. These are people going about their Christmas business. They never mention a stall, because the player may not have built it yet, and they never ask for one.
- **Replays are silent.** Each scene plays once per save.
- **Settings → Story** turns every scene off. Turning it back on starts from the current wish; nothing skipped is replayed.

Lines are speech, never narration. Mechanics stay in each board's lesson line.

## Cast

Bramble, Mr Finch's goat, does not speak.

| Character | Who | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Noelle** | Rosa's granddaughter, reopening her gran's old market for Christmas. The player's friend in Bellmere. | Warm, quick, jokes when she's touched |
| **Theo Marsh** | Sells Christmas trees. Resin on his gloves, needles in his hair. | Dry and contrary, secretly kind |
| **Agnes Holt** | The baker. Flour on her apron. | Pretend-gruff, shows love with food. "There's buns." |
| **Henrik Strand** | The toymaker. Pencil behind his ear. | Tidy, dry, corrects your carpentry |
| **Pip Larkin** | Ten, runs the post. Big satchel. | Full volume, keeps every secret badly |
| **Mr Wren** | An old man who sweeps the square. | Short, gentle, always slightly about something else |
| **Mrs Alder** | Sells cocoa, sings in the choir. | Brisk, generous, sings flat with total confidence |
| **Mr Finch** | Lake fisherman, Bramble's owner. | Slow, deadpan, devoted to his goat |
| **Ada Pike** | Glassblower from across the lake. | Measures things in breaths |
| **Otis Bell** | Keeps the station clock. | Patient, precise |
| **Mary Larkin** | Pip's mum, cook on the coastal steamer. Arrives on Christmas Eve. | Practical, glad |
| **Rosa** | Noelle's gran. Arrives on Christmas Eve. | Warm, mischievous |

**Two light threads**, each said once and paid off once: Pip's mum is trying to get home for Christmas (58, 88, 100), and Noelle's gran will come and see the market (98, 100). Agnes and Henrik bicker through Pip (33) but nothing hangs on it.

## Opening

**Before level 1**

- **Noelle:** You came! Welcome to Bellmere. Gran ran a Christmas market on this square for forty years.
- **Agnes:** Rosa's girl, and a helper, in this snow. Come in when you can't feel your nose. There's buns.
- **Noelle:** It's been empty since she retired. Let's have it open again by Christmas Eve.

## New stalls

**Placed arch · Welcome arch**

- **Theo:** That's an arch. Right. I'll sell my firs under it. Everyone walks through here.
- **Noelle:** That's Theo. He sells the trees, and he argues with everything.
- **Theo:** Not everything. The mulled wine's all right.

**Placed reindeer_stable · Reindeer stable**

- **Pip:** Reindeer! Can I feed them? I've got carrots in both pockets!
- **Noelle:** Go on, Pip. Flat hand, and mind your fingers.
- **Pip:** This one's sniffing my satchel. He thinks the post is for him!

**Placed lumber · Lumber workshop**

- **Theo:** A dry roof for the planks. They won't warp in the snow now.
- **Noelle:** You've got sawdust in your hair, Theo. And pine needles.
- **Theo:** Smells better than a bakery. Don't tell Agnes I said that.

**Placed firework_stall · Firework stall**

- **Pip:** Fireworks! Can I light one? Just a little one?
- **Agnes:** Nobody lights anything before midnight on New Year's Eve, Pip.
- **Pip:** Can I hold the matches, then? No? Can I look at the matches?

**Placed bakery · Gingerbread bakery**

- **Agnes:** A proper oven at last. My kitchen stove was doing forty loaves a day.
- **Agnes:** First gingerbread's yours, love. Don't argue, just eat it.
- **Noelle:** Better do as she says. Nobody's ever won an argument with Agnes.

**Placed chapel · Snowy chapel**

- **Mr Wren:** A sound roof on the chapel. The choir will be glad of it. So will the pigeons.
- **Noelle:** Mr Wren, do you ever put that broom down?
- **Mr Wren:** Not in December, miss. Snow doesn't take a holiday.

**Placed cocoa · Hot cocoa kiosk**

- **Mrs Alder:** Cocoa with cream, and a pinch of cinnamon. First cup's on me, dear.
- **Noelle:** Thank you, Mrs Alder. You're humming already.
- **Mrs Alder:** Carols. Flat as a pancake, they tell me. I don't mind.

**Placed igloo · Ice house**

- **Theo:** Ice from the lake, cut and stacked. It'll keep till spring in here.
- **Agnes:** Good. My butter goes in first, Theo. Then you can have your fish.

**Placed log_cabin · Lakeside smokehouse**

- **Mr Finch:** Smoked trout by Saturday. Bramble, that's not for you.
- **Noelle:** Is that your goat, Mr Finch? She's eating the tinsel.
- **Mr Finch:** She eats everything, miss. She's very fond of Christmas.

**Placed station_kiosk · Station kiosk**

- **Otis:** A kiosk with a clock. Trains run late in the snow, but this clock won't.
- **Pip:** Mr Bell, can I wind it? I'll be ever so careful.
- **Otis:** Sunday mornings, Pip. Seven turns, and not one more.

**Placed toys · Toymaker's workshop**

- **Henrik:** A proper bench, and a window. My soldiers can face the square now.
- **Pip:** Mr Strand, do the drums really play?
- **Henrik:** Every one I make does. Try it. Gently.

**Placed candles · Candlelight shop**

- **Mr Wren:** Beeswax and a little cinnamon. Every window wants a candle in December.
- **Noelle:** You've bought one for every window on the square, Mr Wren?
- **Mr Wren:** And a spare, miss. Somebody always comes home late.

**Placed gifts · Gift wrapping pavilion**

- **Pip:** Can I do the wrapping? I'm brilliant at bows!
- **Noelle:** Pip, that's my parcel you've wrapped. And my mitten.
- **Pip:** It looks festive! You'll get it back on Christmas morning.

**Placed post_office · North Pole post office**

- **Pip:** A real post office, with a stamp that goes clunk! Got any letters?
- **Noelle:** Just the one, for Santa. Which box does it go in?
- **Pip:** The red one. I'll see he gets it. Don't ask how.

**Placed glassblower · Glassblower's stall**

- **Ada:** A furnace of my own. Three breaths for a bauble, five for a big one.
- **Noelle:** Can we watch, Ada? I've never seen glass made.
- **Ada:** Stand back a step. It's hot, it's lovely, and it hates draughts.

**Placed carousel · Reindeer carousel**

- **Pip:** It goes round! Noelle, it actually goes round!
- **Henrik:** Of course it goes round. I made the horses. The reindeer's the fastest.
- **Pip:** Then I'm having the reindeer. Everyone else can have a horse.

**Placed rink · Winter skating rink**

- **Agnes:** I haven't skated since I was a girl. Hold my arm, love. Don't let go.
- **Noelle:** I've got you, Agnes. Look, you're doing it!
- **Agnes:** Don't tell anyone. I've got a reputation.

**Placed santa_workshop · Woodcarvers' hall**

- **Henrik:** Room for carving at last. My old apprentices said they'd come if there was space.
- **Noelle:** There's space now, Henrik. Will they make it for Christmas?
- **Henrik:** If the trains run. I'd better sharpen some chisels.

**Placed tree · Christmas tree**

- **Theo:** There. The best fir I've got, right in the middle of the square.
- **Noelle:** Theo, it's perfect. Did you pick it yourself?
- **Theo:** Walked the whole hill for it. Don't tell anyone I care about trees.

**Placed sleigh · Santa's sleigh**

- **Pip:** A sleigh, with real bells! Mr Wren, have you ever driven one?
- **Mr Wren:** Once or twice, Pip. Only ever on the one night of the year.
- **Pip:** Which night? Mr Wren? Which night?

## Along the way

**Level 3**

- **Pip:** Hello! I'm Pip, I do the post. Are you the new helper? You've got snow on your hat.
- **Noelle:** Pip knows everyone in Bellmere, and what they're all getting for Christmas.
- **Pip:** I never peek! Parcels just rattle at me.

**Level 8**

- **Agnes:** First Sunday of Advent. Noelle, you light the first candle on the wreath.
- **Noelle:** Me? I've only just got here.
- **Agnes:** That's why. New hands bring luck.

**Level 13**

- **Noelle:** Theo, why is there a fir tree in Gran's doorway?
- **Theo:** Every house on the square gets one. It's a market rule.
- **Noelle:** You've just made that up.

**Level 18**

- **Pip:** Leave your boots out tonight! St Nicholas fills them with oranges and nuts.
- **Noelle:** Do you think he'll mind snowy boots, Pip?
- **Pip:** Snowy boots hold more. Everybody knows that.

**Level 23**

- **Mr Wren:** Frost ferns on every window this morning. Clever things.
- **Noelle:** You've been out sweeping since six, Mr Wren. It snowed all night.
- **Mr Wren:** That's why, miss. Somebody has to find the path.

**Level 28**

- **Mrs Alder:** Choir practice tonight, and you're both coming. I need people to stand next to.
- **Noelle:** To help you with the high notes?
- **Mrs Alder:** To stop the others edging away from me. Bring a scarf.

**Level 33**

- **Pip:** Mrs Holt, Mr Strand says your chimney smokes and your bread's burnt.
- **Agnes:** Tell that man it's toasted. On purpose.
- **Pip:** I'm not a carrier pigeon, you know. Well. Sometimes I am.

**Level 38**

- **Pip:** Snowball fight by the fountain! Theo against everyone else!
- **Theo:** Everyone else? That's not a team, Pip. That's the whole town.
- **Pip:** We know. That's how we're going to win.

**Level 43**

- **Mr Finch:** Ice is thick enough on the lake now. Bramble's been out on it since Tuesday.
- **Noelle:** Is that safe for a goat, Mr Finch?
- **Mr Finch:** Safer for her than for the fish, miss.

**Level 48**

- **Agnes:** St Lucia's day. Saffron buns, curled like a cat's tail. Take two.
- **Noelle:** Agnes, you said "take two" yesterday. And the day before.
- **Agnes:** Different buns, love. Every day's different buns.

**Level 53**

- **Theo:** What do you get someone who has everything? Asking for a friend.
- **Noelle:** Who's the friend, Theo?
- **Theo:** Nobody. Forget I asked. Are mittens good? Mittens are good.

**Level 58**

- **Pip:** My mum cooks on the coastal steamer. She sends a postcard from every port.
- **Noelle:** Will she be home for Christmas, Pip?
- **Pip:** She's trying. I don't mind either way. Want a postcard? I've got loads.

**Level 63**

- **Mrs Alder:** Carollers at every door tonight! Open yours and you get a song.
- **Noelle:** And if we don't open it?
- **Mrs Alder:** Then you get two songs, dear. Louder.

**Level 68**

- **Henrik:** A wooden train for the Fenwick boy. The wheels must turn without a squeak.
- **Noelle:** You've been oiling that one wheel for an hour, Henrik.
- **Henrik:** Then it's nearly right.

**Level 73**

- **Pip:** I made a paper chain as long as the square! Mostly red, some green.
- **Noelle:** Pip, is that last week's newspaper?
- **Pip:** It was. The news is festive now.

**Level 78**

- **Ada:** Your window wants a star, Noelle. Blue glass, I think. It catches the evening.
- **Noelle:** Ada, I couldn't. You've been at that all week.
- **Ada:** Seven breaths, it took. It's yours. Hang it high.

**Level 83**

- **Mr Wren:** Shortest day of the year. From tomorrow, a little more light every evening.
- **Noelle:** You make that sound like good news, Mr Wren.
- **Mr Wren:** It's always good news, miss. Every single year.

**Level 88**

- **Pip (shout):** A TELEGRAM! MUM'S ON THE LAST TRAIN ON CHRISTMAS EVE! SHE'S COMING!
- **Agnes:** Then she'll want a warm bun when she steps off. I'll have one ready.
- **Pip:** I'm not crying. It's the snow. It got in my eyes.

**Level 93**

- **Theo:** I've kept a little fir back. For your gran's window, if you want it.
- **Noelle:** Theo Marsh. Did you just do something nice?
- **Theo:** Don't go spreading it about. I've got a reputation too.

**Level 98**

- **Noelle:** Gran's written! She's coming on the last train tomorrow night, with Pip's mum.
- **Pip:** Both of them, on the same train? I'm going to need a bigger sign!

**Level 100**

- **Mary:** Pip! Look how you've grown. And look at this square!
- **Rosa:** All this, in one December? Noelle, love, I'd never have believed it.
- **Noelle:** I didn't do it on my own, Gran. Merry Christmas, everyone.

## Implementation

`tools/prepare_story.py` compiles this script into `lua/story_content.lua` (`--check` detects drift). Scene keys are `before-01`, `after-NN` and `placed-<stall key>`; the bookkeeping is the engine's (`engine/lua/match3/journey.lua`), set up in `lua/journey.lua`. Save version 5 marks every scene up to an older save's current wish as seen, so a returning player continues from where they are.
