# Character attribution

Workers and male/female civilians use **Kenney Animated Characters Survivors
1.1**, released under Creative Commons Zero. The original license is retained
in [kenney-License.txt](kenney-License.txt). Kenney's public source is
<https://kenney.nl/assets/animated-characters-survivors>.

Volcano's source project authored the rescue uniforms, hard hats, tools and
additional work animations. The port retains its idle, eight-direction run,
chop, pickaxe, low/high repair and pet-rescue frames with their original
repetition, FPS and trimmed pivots. Runtime pixels are repacked in `sprites.png`;
no original Rescue Team worker/civilian art or shadow is used.

The editable Blender source, source FBX models and texture authoring tools are
preserved in [source/assets/kenney-characters](../source/assets/kenney-characters/).
They are not runtime dependencies or included in the data bundle. Source declaration
and image hashes are recorded in [asset-catalog.json](asset-catalog.json).

[CHARACTER-PIPELINE.md](CHARACTER-PIPELINE.md) explains how the sheets are rendered
and how to change the characters, animations and textures.
