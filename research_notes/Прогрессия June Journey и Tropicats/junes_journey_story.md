# June's Journey (Wooga): story delivery, post-level flow, and the island

Research date: 2 November 2026.

How far to trust these sources:
- **Direct page reads:** Wooga help centre, Naavik, Vocal transcripts, Mobi.gg, the Daymude review and PocketGamer.biz.
- **Search excerpts only:** the Fandom wiki (HTTP 402), AppGamer, TV Tropes and GamesBeat (HTTP 403) refused direct fetches. Their claims come from search excerpts, so check them before relying on any single detail. The search tool summarised several AppGamer threads together, so a specific claim may belong to a neighbouring thread rather than the exact one linked.
- **No first-hand observation:** I did not play the game or watch a video. No source states the exact on-screen order after a scene, so that order is marked as a reconstruction.

## Exactly what happens on screen after a scene is completed

### Takeaway
The end of a scene is an economy payout, not a story moment: score, suitcase rewards, Mastery points, and a Star Box if a new Star was earned. Story is tied to clues, which come from a first play and from a replay after the third Star. The exact order of screens is not documented in anything I could read.

### Cited Findings
- During play, finding some items makes a suitcase fly to the top of the screen. Its reward is revealed "when the scene's score is calculated at the end". Faster finds score higher, and wrong taps cost points — [Wooga help: Hidden Object Scene](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-a-hidden-object-scene-439)
- End-of-scene points accumulate across plays toward five Mastery Stars per scene, shown in the Scene Browser, and each Star costs more than the last — [Wooga help: How do I play](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/how-do-i-play-junes-journey-394)
- A new Star awards a Star Box, and every fifth Star Box is a special 5-Star Box (search excerpt) — [Wooga help: Stars](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-do-i-need-stars-for-and-how-do-i-get-them-311)
- The first clue is found on the first play of a new scene. The second appears when the scene is played after its third Star is earned. Clues are required to advance the story — [Wooga help: Hidden Object Scene](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-a-hidden-object-scene-439)
- Tapping skips the post-scene animation and the player still gets all rewards (community answer, undated in the excerpt) — [AppGamer](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/28047-after-we-play-a-scene-can-we-skip-t)
- Scenes pay Coins and Star progress. Coins buy decorations, and decorations give Flowers, which unlock scenes — [Naavik, Nov 2023 republication of an Oct 2022 piece](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/junes-journey-hidden-object-game/)
- The Scene Browser is the chapter hub. The Journal sits at its far left and the Adventure Scene card at its far right — [Wooga help: Journal](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-the-journal-and-what-do-i-do-with-it-312); [Wooga help: Adventure Scene](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-an-adventure-scene-483)

### Inferences
- **Reconstructed order, not confirmed as a sequence:**
  1. Last object found.
  2. Score tally and suitcase reveal.
  3. Mastery bar fill, plus Star Box if a Star was reached.
  4. Clue plus dialogue, on qualifying plays only.
  5. Return to the Scene Browser for the chapter.
- The player appears to land on the chapter hub rather than the island. No source states this directly. The island is a separate destination reached when Flowers or Coins are needed.
- Most plays deliver no story. A scene has at most two clues, so replays are economy and Mastery grinding, and story arrives only at the first play and the third-Star threshold.
- **Worth copying:** keep the payout short and skippable, and fire narrative only at clue thresholds, so replays never repeat dialogue.

### Gaps
- Whether clue dialogue plays before or after the score screen. The transcripts place a clue marker before each dialogue block, but they are static text and do not show timing.
- Whether anything auto-routes the player to the island after a scene. Short gameplay footage would settle this and the point above.

## When cutscenes and dialogues play, what form they take, and how long they are

### Takeaway
The main story is short scripted dialogue attached to individual clues, about 3-10 labelled lines per clue and 50-70 lines per chapter, roughly 1,000 words in the one chapter measured. A second, lighter channel plays Mr. Talbot's dialogue on landmark upgrades. I found no source that describes the visual format or confirms voice acting.

### Cited Findings
- **Chapter 1 transcript:** 9 clues and 49 labelled lines (June 28, Virginia 20, Mrs. Talbot 1), plus a little italic narration — [Vocal transcript, Ch. 1](https://vocal.media/fiction/june-s-journey-full-story)
- **Chapter 22 transcript:** 10 clues, 70 labelled lines and 74 total lines, with 3-10 labelled lines per clue — [Vocal transcript, Ch. 22](https://vocal.media/fiction/june-s-journey-full-story-atgy1a0e2k)
- **Chapter 24 transcript:** 10 clues and 56 labelled lines, about 900-1,000 words of story text. A short block follows Clue 10 — [Vocal transcript, Ch. 24](https://vocal.media/fiction/june-s-journey-full-story-4a2x1053f)
- The transcripts are fan-made and undated beyond "3 years ago" on one page, so they may not match the current build. Each line carries a speaker name.
- Naavik counts 6,050 dialogues across 275 chapters at the time of writing — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/junes-journey-hidden-object-game/)
- Mr. Talbot's dialogues follow landmark upgrades, and a community answer says the game cannot replay them (old answer) — [AppGamer](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/45973-can-i-rewatch-the-dialogues-with-mr)
- Solving an Adventure Scene continues the chapter story. It is an interactive puzzle with no time limit and a single solution — [Wooga help: Adventure Scene](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-an-adventure-scene-483)
- The Journal gives a summary of each chapter and shows all collected clues — [Wooga help: Journal](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-the-journal-and-what-do-i-do-with-it-312)
- Completing a character card photo gives one energy bar and "a bit of backstory" — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/junes-journey-hidden-object-game/)
- A player reviewer says they usually skip dialogue because it takes too long to read — [AppGamer reviews](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/reviews/)
- Wooga also runs story outside the game, including a podcast, extra fiction and behind-the-scenes videos — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/junes-journey-hidden-object-game/). The podcast is listed as [The Lost Diaries](https://castbox.fm/channel/3924643). A novel, *Death Wasn't Invited*, is an original story rather than a game adaptation, published in 2026 — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/inside-junes-journeys-leap-from-mobile-game-to-bestselling-novel/)

### Inferences
- 6,050 / 275 is 22 "dialogues" per chapter. That is more than the 9-10 clues per chapter, so Naavik's "dialogue" unit likely includes moments beyond clues, such as Adventure Scene steps. This is my calculation and interpretation.
- At an assumed 200-250 words per minute, about 1,000 words is 4-5 minutes of reading per chapter, or 25-40 seconds per clue. The reading speed is my assumption.
- Lines per clue stay low enough to read between rounds, and the chapter-level length comes from clue count rather than long scenes.
- **Worth copying:** 3-8 line beats, a named speaker on every line, a chapter recap in a Journal, and side dialogue tied to island upgrades.

### Gaps
- **Visual format is not sourced.** From memory, the game uses character bust portraits with text bubbles over scene art and no voice-over. No source I could read states this, so treat it as unverified until checked against footage.
- Dialogue skip behaviour in the current build, and whether a skip button exists for unseen lines.
- Replay of clue dialogue. A search summary mentioned that tapping a clue in the chapter book replays its dialogue, but I could not find that text in the page I checked. Wooga's Journal article mentions clues and summaries, not replay.

## How a chapter's plot is built, with worked examples

### Takeaway
The template is rigid: 5 Hidden Object Scenes, 2 clues each (10 beats), then 1 Adventure Scene that opens the next hook. Clues work as a physical evidence chain, and chapters end on a reversal or an open threat. Chapter 1 confirms this, with one scene carrying a single clue so the first session moves faster.

### Cited Findings
**Structure**
- Each chapter has five Hidden Object Scenes and a final Adventure Scene — [Wooga help](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/how-do-i-play-junes-journey-394)
- A scene unlocks with a Flower level plus both clues from the previous scene. For example, Scene 3 needs Flower Level 3 and both Scene 2 clues — [Wooga help: locked scene](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/why-is-a-hidden-object-scene-locked-315)
- The Adventure Scene needs a Star count plus all chapter clues — [Wooga help: Adventure Scene](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-an-adventure-scene-483)
- The next chapter needs the Adventure Scene completed plus a required Flower level — [Wooga help: next chapter](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/how-do-i-unlock-the-next-chapter-316)

### Inferences
- **Chapter beat template:**
  - Setup carried over from the last hook (clues 1-2).
  - Evidence chain with one object per beat (clues 3-6).
  - Complication or action (clues 7-9).
  - Reversal at clue 10.
  - Adventure Scene as the discovery and new hook.
- Clue objects are the plot. Each one is a thing visible in the scene, which is why a hidden-object loop can carry a mystery without cutscenes.
- Locations follow the plot, so a chapter's 5-6 scenes are 5-6 stops in one continuous movement, as in estate parlor, garden gate, lane, gatehouse.
- Chapter 1's single-clue opening scene looks like a tutorial shortcut. That is my reading; the wiki notes only that the level is unusual.

### Gaps
- Plot beats for Chapter 2 scenes L8-L10.5, because the wiki's full text was not reachable.
- The Flower gate value per scene or chapter. That belongs with the economy researcher.

## The overall plot, how arcs conclude, and what a player sees at the content edge

### Takeaway
June Parker investigates the deaths of her sister Clare and brother-in-law Harry in the 1920s. The first mystery resolves early, with Sam Watts confessing around Chapter 6, and the serial then moves through linked capers. Volumes end on chapter-level closures and carry a volume reward. At the content edge the player simply waits for the weekly release, and I found no evidence of a "to be continued" screen.

### Cited Findings
**Premise and cast**
- June is a British WWI veteran nurse who goes to New York over a reported murder-suicide — [Daymude review, Oct 2017](https://jdaymude.github.io/review/videogame-junes-journey/)
- Key characters on the wiki: [June Parker](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/June_Parker), [Clare van Buren](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Clare_van_Buren), Harry van Buren, Virginia van Buren (their daughter), housekeeper Martha Talbot and groundskeeper [Walter Talbot](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Walter_Talbot), who helped raise June and Clare, FBI agent [Sam Watts](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Sam_Watts), [Eddie Daggett](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Eddie_Daggett), and [Jack Hayes](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Jack_Hayes)

**First arc (spoilers, wiki excerpts)**
- Sam Watts, the investigating FBI agent, is Virginia's biological father and Clare's former partner. He shot Clare in a confrontation, staged it as Harry's act, framed Eddie Daggett and later killed him under cover of self-defence — [Sam Watts](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Sam_Watts); [Eddie Daggett](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Eddie_Daggett)
- He pleads guilty to the murders of Clare, Harry and Eddie — [wiki excerpt](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Sam's_Confession_-_Level_30,_Vol._1)
- The opening chapter order is Arrival in New York, Speak Easy, Eddie Daggett, Clare's Secret, Havana, Finding Virginia — [AppGamer guide excerpt](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/43955-first-book-starts-with-june-being-o)
- Chapter 7 begins a new adventure about June helping Virginia accept the loss of her parents — [wiki](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/June_and_Virginia_-_Chapter_7,_Vol._1)
- **Conflict in my excerpts:** one summary called [Estate Garage, L20](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Estate_Garage_-_Level_20,_Vol._1) the end of the homicide investigation and the start of Virginia's kidnapping plot, yet [Sam's Confession](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Sam's_Confession_-_Level_30,_Vol._1) is at L30. The summary also called L20 the final Volume 1 level, which is clearly wrong because Volume 1 runs to L500.

**Later arcs**
- Chapter 13 brings June back to Orchid Island and starts a new adventure in New Orleans — [wiki](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Mrs._Talbot's_Son_-_Chapter_13,_Vol._1)
- Chapters 22-24 run a Marquess and Fabergé egg caper in Europe with Irene and Interpol's Detective Wendel. - After June is shot by a hired killer and nearly dies, she confesses her love to Jack (search excerpt, chapter not given) — [Jack Hayes](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Jack_Hayes)

**Volumes and endings**
- Volume sizes are 100, 50, 25, 35 and 35 chapters, with Volume 6 at Chapter 30 when Naavik counted 275 total. The original plan was 100 chapters, and a second volume followed its success — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/junes-journey-hidden-object-game/)

**Content edge**

Flower level can run past released scenes (community answers, latest dated 2023) — [AppGamer](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/38475-why-do-i-have-to-wait-6-days-to-go)

- Secrets events are still being scheduled, with one starting 2 Nov 2026 — [GamersUnite](https://www.gamersunite.com/june-s-journey-hidden-object-mystery-game/themes/june-s-journey-hidden-objects-secrets). This shows live operations continue but says nothing about the chapter schedule.

### Inferences
- If weekly releases never stopped, the count should now be about 467 (295 + about 172 weeks from July 2023 to November 2026). This is my arithmetic, and it suggests Mobi's 350+ table is stale or that the cadence changed. Confirm in-game before quoting either number.
- From numbering at 5 levels per chapter, L30 is the end of Chapter 6, "Finding Virginia", so the founding mystery ends about 6 chapters in. Later plots keep the cast and investigator format rather than extending one whodunit.
- From the Chapter 22 transcript, Irene appears to be June's mother, who disappeared. This fits the argument over her absence but is not stated in my excerpts.
- The edge state appears to be a locked next chapter with a countdown, based on player questions asking why they must wait a number of days. No source describes the screen.
- **Worth copying:** resolve the first mystery fast, keep the cast and home base, and let each chapter end on a hook. Since each chapter is data only, content can ship without a client update.

## Island/Estate: story-tied versus cosmetic, and which unlocks which

### Takeaway
Building unlocks story, through Flowers. I found no case of a story beat unlocking a standard building in the main path, apart from volume-completion rewards. Most of the island is cosmetic Flower and Coin generation. The story connection is concentrated in the Estate, the Talbots, the Lighthouse and other Landmarks, and Memoirs.

### Cited Findings
**Direction of the gate**
- Building, upgrading, decorating and renovating landmarks earn Flowers, which gate scenes and chapters — [Wooga help](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/how-do-i-unlock-the-next-chapter-316)
- A 2017 reviewer criticised this as story progress tied to building upgrades, stretching content through a chain of currencies — [Daymude](https://jdaymude.github.io/review/videogame-junes-journey/)

**Story-tied elements**
- **The Estate:** a building on Orchid Island belonging to June and Virginia. It generates one coin every 10 minutes up to 60, and its style can be changed — [wiki: Estate](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Estate). It is also the Chapter 1 opening location — [wiki: L1](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Orchid_Island_Estate_-_Level_1,_Vol._1)
- **The Talbots' House:** a 3x3 shop building available at level 5, named after two story characters. No plot use appeared in my excerpts — [wiki: Buildings](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Buildings)
- **Lighthouse:** the first of five landmarks that Mr. Talbot restores, on the south coast. Level 1 is a one-task tutorial, later levels have eight tasks, and it is fully restored at level 20. Tasks pay Flowers, and full restoration gives one energy per 40 minutes — [wiki: Lighthouse](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Lighthouse); [Landmarks](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Landmarks)
- **Lighthouse lore:** Talbot's lines cover Captain Kidd, supposed buried treasure, a privateering cove and rum barrels from the island's bootlegging history — [wiki: Lighthouse](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Lighthouse)
- **Landmark timing:** each first-landmark level takes about 2 weeks, and all four landmarks take about two years, with a fifth providing endless progression (2022-23 data; the wiki also counts five landmarks) — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/junes-journey-hidden-object-game/)
- **Story location overlap:** Chapter 13 includes a level called "The Old Lighthouse" — [wiki](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Mrs._Talbot's_Son_-_Chapter_13,_Vol._1)
- **Memoirs:** Gacha card collections unlock permanent island features such as dolphins, whales, a sailing boat, a coastal wedding and a beach cove — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/junes-journey-hidden-object-game/)
- **"Return to Orchid Island":** a memoir event from 29 Nov 2021 in which June explores island memories with Martha Talbot, with album pages including the Lighthouse, Talbot's Kitchen and Shed — [wiki](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Return_to_Orchid_Island)
- **Character backgrounds:** reached through a book icon at the lower left of the island view, along with card collections — [Wooga help: Journal](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-the-journal-and-what-do-i-do-with-it-312)
- **Residents:** the wiki has a category of [Orchid Island Residents](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Orchid_Island_Residents), which includes Jack Hayes

**Purely functional or cosmetic**
- Shop buildings are decorations giving Flowers by level, and can only be placed on Orchid Island — [wiki: Buildings](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Buildings)
- Some buildings generate Coins and most can be upgraded for more Flowers — [Wooga help](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/how-do-i-play-junes-journey-394)
- A September 2020 community count gave 29 coin-producing 3x3 buildings, the last being the Summer House. A later answer said 28 including the Estate — [AppGamer](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/34172-how-can-you-get-more-buildings)
- Island building has 1920s-themed decorations and limited-time items, and players can visit friends — [Julia's Bookcase review](https://www.juliasbookcase.com/blog/junes-journey-game-review)

### Inferences
- The island is the story's home, not its stage. Named characters and places give it meaning, but most main-chapter plot happens in painted scenes, and building placement has no plot effect in anything I read.
- Landmarks are the best pattern to copy. They give a named character, a slow restoration, and lore lines separate from the main plot, which keeps a side story running while chapters are gated.
- **Worth copying:** one home estate that is also the first chapter's location, 1-2 caretaker characters who run upgrades, and a volume-end reward decoration. Keep the main plot from depending on the player's layout.

- A full list of Landmarks after the Lighthouse, and the current status of Snowdrop Island and other islands.
- Whether character houses exist beyond the Talbots' House, and whether residents appear on the island map.

## Narrative content per hour of play, and how often a new story beat arrives

### Takeaway
No source gives narrative per hour, so only estimates are possible. Per chapter there are 9-10 clue beats plus an Adventure Scene, about 4-5 minutes of reading. A caught-up player gets about one chapter a week, roughly 1,000 words, while a new player's rate is set by the Flower and Star gates.

### Cited Findings
- 5 scenes with 2 clues each, with the second clue held behind the third Star — [Wooga help](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-a-hidden-object-scene-439)
- One new chapter a week, every week since 2018 at the time of writing — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/junes-journey-hidden-object-game/)
- 1,375+ hidden object scenes, 275 puzzle scenes and 6,050 dialogues over 275 chapters, which is roughly 5.5 years of weekly content — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/junes-journey-hidden-object-game/)
- There is no time limit or loss in a hidden object round, and a free hint is available every 30 seconds — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/junes-journey-hidden-object-game/)
- Retention is about 16.5% at day 30 and 15% at day 60, with only 0.75% lost from day 60 to day 180, which Naavik ties to episode cadence — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/junes-journey-hidden-object-game/)
- A caught-up chapter is reachable only weekly, and some players stretch it by replaying one scene a day — [AppGamer](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/38475-why-do-i-have-to-wait-6-days-to-go)
- Writers were reportedly on Chapter 60 at launch, with more than a year mapped, and lead writer Rebecca Harwick added shorter side stories alongside the main mystery — [GamerBoom excerpt](https://gamerboom.com/?p=95439). The excerpt dates this launch to October 2022, which conflicts with the game's 2017 release, so treat the date as an error and the claim as unverified.
- Help Wanted cases have waits of 3, 8 or 16 hours — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/junes-journey-hidden-object-game/). These gameplay cases are not story, per [Wooga's 2018 post](https://www.wooga.com/blog/help-wanted).

### Inferences
- **Beat pacing:** each scene's first play gives a beat immediately. The second beat waits for the third Star, and the next scene stays locked until both clues are found, so the rhythm within a scene is one quick beat, a replay grind, then a second beat. The grind length depends on Star thresholds, which I did not find.
- **Weekly volume for a caught-up player:** about 9-10 clue beats, about 50-70 lines and about 4-5 minutes of reading per week. My calculation, assuming a typical chapter.
- The serial model depends on a lot of reading spread thinly over much replaying, so the story's job is to give each replay grind a near-term reward.
