# Tropicats (Wooga): story delivery and island-building structure

Source quality note: no independent design deconstruction (Deconstructor of Fun, GameRefinery, Naavik) of Tropicats was found. The best evidence is Wooga's own support centre (exact mechanics), Wooga's blog (developer intent), the App Store listing, and two small third-party texts. No video walkthrough or gameplay capture was watched, so anything about on-screen order is limited. Labels used: CONFIRMED = stated by a source; INFERRED = my reading; UNVERIFIED = seen only in a search snippet, because the full page would not load.

## Is the "houses were just random buildings, not story-specific" memory right? What can be built?

### Takeaway
The memory is half right. Tropicats has two separate layers: story buildings (huts and similar) that are rebuilt through Chief's Diary tasks using objects from marked levels, and free decorations bought from a shop. The second layer is the "random" part the client remembers, but even it is often themed to the story or a character.

### Cited Findings
**Layer 1: story buildings (CONFIRMED)**
- "Rebuilding is part of the story", with different objects needed for different buildings, and the levels that award those objects are marked — [Support: How do I rebuild buildings?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/how-do-i-rebuild-buildings-305)
- Once a rebuilding task listed in the Chief's Diary is completed, the building appears in the village — [same article](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/how-do-i-rebuild-buildings-305)
- "As the story progresses, destroyed huts and other buildings will become available for you to rebuild", and each one then yields coins or diamonds — [Support: Let's rebuild the village!](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/lets-rebuild-the-village-304)
- Certain buildings give diamonds hourly when tapped; the article does not name them — [Support: diamonds (FR)](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats/articles/comment-faire-pour-obtenir-des-diamants-318)
- A guide tells players that advancing the story means focusing on "the quests and collecting the requisite components to complete each area" — [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/tropicats-tips-cheats-and-strategies/)

**Layer 2: shop decorations (CONFIRMED)**
- "Objects and decorations" from the shop in the lower left corner earn Village points that raise village rank — [Support: Let's rebuild the village!](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/lets-rebuild-the-village-304)
- A player review describes houses, trees, flowers and decorations bought with coins and gems; these raise village rank and cat happiness — [Mog Anarchy review, Jan 2018](http://moganarchy.blogspot.com/2018/01/review-tropicats.html)
- Buying with coins or gems is also described in a 2018 store review (decorate "by trading coins or gems for items") — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1215220850)
- Blue diamonds buy "premium village decor" — [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/tropicats-tips-cheats-and-strategies/)

**Decorations are not actually random in intent (CONFIRMED)**
- Decorations are usually themed to "something in the story, or to a particular character". Example given: Elsie's hut matches the colour of her favourite outfit — [Wooga blog, Aug 2018](https://www.wooga.com/blog/tropicats-behind-the-scenes-how-characters-are-created-a5c053db4922)
- The Impurrials faction has a British flavour with "super tidy gardens", so art follows faction identity — [same post](https://www.wooga.com/blog/tropicats-behind-the-scenes-how-characters-are-created-a5c053db4922)

**Free placement (CONFIRMED for moving; partial for rules)**
- The guide advises using waiting time to "build, reposition, and otherwise design your town", so items can be moved — [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/tropicats-tips-cheats-and-strategies/)
- Workshop items are also explicitly placed by the player: "You have one week to place all 4 deco items on your island" — [Support: Ava's Workshop](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/what-is-avas-workshop-346)

**Other buildable sources and scale (CONFIRMED except where marked)**
- Ava's Workshop: four deco items built from blueprints found in Mystery Boxes; a weekly reset wipes blueprints and unplaced items — [Support: Ava's Workshop](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/what-is-avas-workshop-346)
- Seasonal Sets: limited-time sets of buildings, flowers, animals and paths, themed to a holiday, season or a Tropicat's favourite hobby. They cost tokens rather than coins or diamonds and pay out a Grand Prix — [Support: Seasonal Sets (FR)](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats/articles/lots-saisonniers-392)
- Launch scope was "more than 100 buildings" to decorate with (UNVERIFIED, snippet of the launch post; the page would not load) — [Wooga launch post](https://blog.wooga.com/tropicats-is-woogas-new-puzzle-game-4660e03f196e)
- An August 2018 update promised "dozens of new decorations to buy and build" alongside two chapters — [Wooga blog](https://www.wooga.com/blog/tropicats-behind-the-scenes-how-a-match-3-level-is-made-977a77ca9b4b)
- Named examples available in sources: Elsie's hut (story/character building), Elsie's cabin rooms (Collections event, below), waterfalls and flamingos (decor seen via a store-screenshot search summary, UNVERIFIED) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1215220850); [Support: Collections (FR)](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats/articles/que-sont-les-collections-393)

### Inferences
- The client's memory probably reflects the much larger, visually dominant shop catalogue. The story layer exists but is a smaller set of huts. The share of each could not be measured.
- Story buildings likely sit at fixed positions, because the support text says the building "can be found in the village" after a task rather than being placed. This is not stated outright.
- Clone guidance: copy the split. Fixed story buildings carry progress and income; free decor carries self-expression and rank.

### Gaps
- No full catalogue or building list found. Beyond Elsie's hut, no story building names could be established.
- Whether one item has variants or skins: no source describes per-item variants. Seasonal sets add new items, but that is not the same thing.
- Whether story buildings can be moved, and whether placement snaps to a grid. Gamezebo mentions dark green patches and regular grassy areas, but that passage is about match-3 tiles, not village placement.

## What happens on screen after a level completes?

### Takeaway
The exact order could not be established from text sources. The parts are confirmed (level reward, return to island, Chief's Diary task, building appears, dialogue exists), but their sequence is inferred. A capture of real play is needed to confirm it.

### Cited Findings
- Levels award coins and gems, then players spend them on the island — [Mog Anarchy](http://moganarchy.blogspot.com/2018/01/review-tropicats.html)
- Levels that reward quest objects are marked — [Support: rebuild buildings](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/how-do-i-rebuild-buildings-305)
- The game returns to the island after a level: friend-invite rewards appear after completing a level, "once you're back on your island" (paraphrase of support text) — [Support: Tell a Friend (FR)](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats/articles/invite-tes-amis-373)
- A notebook at the bottom of the screen shows the current quest and can jump directly to the next required level — [Support: I don't know what to do next (FR)](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats/articles/je-ne-sais-pas-ce-que-je-dois-faire-328)
- Diamonds are awarded for completing levels, quests and chapters, and for each new village rank — [Support: diamonds (FR)](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats/articles/comment-faire-pour-obtenir-des-diamants-318)

### Inferences
- Likely flow (INFERRED, not observed): win screen with coins and possibly a quest object → island → Diary task completes when objects are collected → building appears → dialogue or cutscene → next task and a shortcut to the next marked level. Decorating is optional and happens outside this chain, and Gamezebo's advice to build while waiting for lives supports that.

### Gaps
- No source gives the real order, whether dialogue comes before or after the building appears, or whether the player must tap to build or it happens automatically. The support wording suggests it just appears.

## When do cutscenes and dialogues play, how long are they, and what is the plot?

### Takeaway
The story is a comedic ensemble mystery delivered through hand-drawn cutscenes and cat dialogue. It opens with the totem being cut down and an earthquake wrecking the island, with the player as village chief hunting the culprit. Exact trigger points and durations are not documented.

### Cited Findings
**Plot**
- An earthquake devastates the island after the protective magic totem is cut down by an unknown vandal. The player is village chief, finding the culprit and rebuilding — [Mog Anarchy](http://moganarchy.blogspot.com/2018/01/review-tropicats.html)
- Wooga's launch post says the totem is "mercilessly cut down" and refers to "beautiful hand drawn cutscenes" (UNVERIFIED, snippet only) — [Wooga launch post](https://blog.wooga.com/tropicats-is-woogas-new-puzzle-game-4660e03f196e)
- The island story contains "broken totems to missing shamans to secret loves" — [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/tropicats-tips-cheats-and-strategies/)
- The store listing frames the arc as "the mystery of the Great Totem" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1215220850)
- Later content: a store listing snippet says "Move to a brand new island, restore Felicity's Farm and solve the island's spooky mystery" (UNVERIFIED, snippet only; the current listing does not contain it) — [search result for older App Store listing](https://itunes.apple.com/ae/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850?mt=8)

**Characters**
- Olivia, clever gardener; Elsie, brave assistant; Kip, Elsie's annoying little brother — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1215220850)
- Ava appears in UI category names ("Ava's Purse", "Ava's Workshop"), and "Elsie's Boosters" is another category. Ava seems to front the shop and workshop — [Support index](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats)
- Felicity (from "Felicity's Farm", UNVERIFIED) and Lily ("Lily said she wanted to keep them as pets" in a 2021 patch note) — [App Store AE](https://apps.apple.com/ae/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850)
- The Impurrials are an antagonist-flavoured British-styled faction, "dastardly" and "hoarding chests of booty" in a pirate event (the event text is UNVERIFIED, snippet only) — [Wooga blog](https://www.wooga.com/blog/tropicats-behind-the-scenes-how-characters-are-created-a5c053db4922); [search result for older App Store listing](https://itunes.apple.com/ae/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850?mt=8)
- Blacktail is a pirate-costumed cat who is really Furbert, a Duchess's highborn son who ran away to play pirate and fears his mother; sample line "shiver me timpurrs". Elsie is "our star gossip queen and fashionista". Unnamed cast includes an older Highland cat, the Duchess, an unstable male shaman, a stoic dog and a brash Highlander — [Wooga blog, Apr 2019](https://www.wooga.com/blog/woogas-cyrus-nemati-on-tropicat-humor-61f4ff125a56)
- Writer Cyrus Nemati: the cast has about 30 characters, each with distinct goals and needs. He draws a character matrix at the start of each new chapter, and conflict between mismatched characters drives the comedy. The premise is cats used to a charmed, isolated life being forced to adapt and resisting it — [same post](https://www.wooga.com/blog/woogas-cyrus-nemati-on-tropicat-humor-61f4ff125a56)
- Character design starts from a writer's description (age, job, look, personality), and the team acts out expressions for the artist — [Wooga blog, Aug 2018](https://www.wooga.com/blog/tropicats-behind-the-scenes-how-characters-are-created-a5c053db4922)
- A player singles out each cat's distinct personality and their dialogue with you and each other — [Mog Anarchy](http://moganarchy.blogspot.com/2018/01/review-tropicats.html)

**Player response**
- Reviews cite the story as a retention driver: restoring the neighbourhood and unfolding the story kept one player engaged (2020), and another worried they might never learn how the story ends (2019) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1215220850)

### Inferences
- Dialogue appears to be the main continuous delivery, with cutscenes reserved for larger beats. Sources mention both but none states the ratio.
- Dialogue is probably organised by chapter, given the writer's per-chapter process.

### Gaps
- Duration of cutscenes and dialogues: no source. Do not quote a number.
- Trigger points (per task, per building, per chapter): no source.
- Whether the culprit is revealed, and how the Great Totem arc resolves: not found.
- Player-character identity conflicts slightly: support says "Chief's Diary" in English, while the French version was summarised as "Captain's journal" in one article and "Chief's journal" in another. The English "Chief" matches the review's village chief role.

## How is content sectioned, and what does the end of content look like?

### Takeaway
Content is structured as levels → chapters → islands, with quests and "areas" inside. End of content is an open-ended waiting state rather than an ending, and the game has been in legacy mode for some time with little sign of new content.

### Cited Findings
- Chapters are the release unit: an August 2018 update added 2 chapters and 80 levels, bringing the total to 240 — [Wooga blog](https://www.wooga.com/blog/tropicats-behind-the-scenes-how-a-match-3-level-is-made-977a77ca9b4b)
- Chapters pay a diamond reward — [Support: diamonds (FR)](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats/articles/comment-faire-pour-obtenir-des-diamants-318)
- Islands: Tropicat Island's theme was "thoroughly explored", and new islands would draw on other cultures — [Wooga blog](https://www.wooga.com/blog/tropicats-behind-the-scenes-how-characters-are-created-a5c053db4922)
- A 2019 review mentions "diamonds from islands" taking longer, implying several islands existed by then, and another reports expecting to stop at level 1295 — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1215220850)
- Each level has easy, medium and hard variants, and hard levels can be replayed for coins, diamonds and tournament points — [Mog Anarchy](http://moganarchy.blogspot.com/2018/01/review-tropicats.html)
- End state: the support article congratulates players who finished all levels, possibly three times, and says new levels are coming very soon, with no schedule — [Support: new levels (FR)](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats/articles/quand-y-aura-t-il-de-nouveaux-niveaux-319)
- A 2018 reviewer reports mastering every level and waiting for an update, noting that replays no longer gave bonus rewards — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1215220850)
- Blog comments mention new levels no longer arriving (user reports, undated in my reading) — [Mog Anarchy comments](http://moganarchy.blogspot.com/2018/01/review-tropicats.html)

**Shutdown status: NOT shut down, as far as evidence shows**
- The App Store still lists version 2.5.11, dated 12 Oct (year not shown), with recent notes dated 2025 such as 2.2.3 of 09/11/2025. Date formats on the page are ambiguous — [App Store AE](https://apps.apple.com/ae/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850)
- Mobilegamer.biz (14 May 2026) calls it a "legacy release" earning a negligible amount in the last 30 days, per AppMagic estimates — [Mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/another-round-of-layoffs-at-playtika-owned-wooga/)
- Launch was November 2017, after soft launch; Wooga's marketing lead said they then started small in the US — [Apptamin](https://www.apptamin.com/blog/app-preview-videos-tropicats/); [deutsche-startups](https://www.deutsche-startups.de/2018/06/18/wooga-umsatz-schrumpft-aber-endlich-wieder-gewinne/?amp=1)

### Inferences
- The undated entries 2.5.1 (28 May), 2.5.8 (25 Aug) and 2.5.11 (12 Oct) sit above 2025-dated versions, so they are most likely from 2026. That would mean the game was still being patched in October 2026.
- Recent version notes are a repeated bug-fix joke, which suggests maintenance rather than new story. Whether new chapters are still shipping could not be confirmed.
- There is apparently no authored "the end": the design treats the last level as a pause.

### Gaps
- Number of islands, their names beyond Tropicat Island and Felicity's Farm (UNVERIFIED), levels per island, and current level total. Level 1295 is one reviewer's 2019 figure and may not be a cap.
- What happens to the old island when moving on, and whether the player can return.

## What makes the island motivating?

### Takeaway
Motivation comes from visible restoration, village rank rewards, passive income from buildings, cat wishes, and limited-time decor. Social features are light: there is no evidence of island visits, only invites, a share camera and tournaments.

### Cited Findings
- Village rank: decorating earns points, a new rank pays rewards, and the rank is shown top right with the next reward on tap — [Support: Village Rank (FR)](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats/articles/qu-est-ce-que-le-rang-de-village-321)
- Rebuilt buildings pay coins or diamonds, and some pay hourly on tap — [Support: Let's rebuild](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/lets-rebuild-the-village-304); [Support: diamonds (FR)](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats/articles/comment-faire-pour-obtenir-des-diamants-318)
- Cat happiness rises with decoration (details not explained) — [Mog Anarchy](http://moganarchy.blogspot.com/2018/01/review-tropicats.html)
- "MAKE A WISH – Play matching games and win gifts to make the cats' dreams come true!" — [App Store JM](https://apps.apple.com/jm/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850)
- Collections: a limited-time event where card packs furnish each room of Elsie's cabin, and each room reflects Tropicat personalities — [Support: Collections (FR)](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats/articles/que-sont-les-collections-393)
- Seasonal Sets and Ava's Workshop add time pressure on decor (see above).
- Tropicam: photograph the village and send it to friends — [Support: Tropicam (FR)](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats/articles/tropicam-332)
- Friends: invites pay up to 3 rewards a day; the article mentions no visits — [Support: Tell a Friend (FR)](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats/articles/invite-tes-amis-373)
- Tournaments: online competition on making requested special fruit, with top 10 prizes — [Mog Anarchy](http://moganarchy.blogspot.com/2018/01/review-tropicats.html)
- Events listed: Treasure Dive, Pirate Breakout, Mega Boost, Challenges — [Support index](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/fr/tropicats)
- Marketing led with match-3 plus building, closing on a "cat paradise" image; that video beat the alternatives, though the confidence levels were modest (73 and 84%) — [Apptamin](https://www.apptamin.com/blog/app-preview-videos-tropicats/)

### Inferences
- Copy priority for the clone: restoration tied to story, rank rewards, hourly tap income, then themed limited-time decor. Island visits appear unnecessary, as Tropicats shows no sign of them.
- "Cat rescue" and cat-visitor collecting do not appear in any source. The closest is restoring the village for existing cats plus wishes.

### Gaps
- Whether cats walk around the island or react to decorations, and how "wishes" work (a store-copy line only). Mechanics for these need a capture.
- How cat happiness affects anything, if at all.
- Absence of friend visits is only absence of evidence: support articles and the listing do not mention them.
