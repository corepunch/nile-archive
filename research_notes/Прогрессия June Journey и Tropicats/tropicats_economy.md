# Tropicats (Wooga) progression economy

Evidence tags: **[official]** = Wooga help centre, Wooga blog or the App Store IAP list. **[player]** = store review or player blog, one person, often years old. **[third-party]** = aggregator or guide site. **[estimate]** = my arithmetic, not a sourced number. **[wiki-measured]** is not used anywhere: I found no readable wiki.

The official help centre describes systems but almost never gives amounts. The exact loop structure is well sourced. The exact payout and cost tables are not, and no number for them is invented here.

## Status: is the game still live?

### Takeaway
Tropicats is live, not shut down, as of 2 November 2026. It still gets store updates, but it is a legacy title with negligible revenue, so treat it as being in maintenance.

### Cited Findings

## What does finishing one level pay?

### Takeaway
A win pays **coins and diamonds**, not stars, and no per-win amount is published. Paws exist but come from Challenges, not from ordinary level wins. Harder tiers pay more. Part of the diamond reward goes into a paid piggy bank, the Treasure Chest.

### Cited Findings
- [official] Coins come from completing levels, reaching a new Village Rank and spinning the Wheel of Fortune; some buildings also give coins when tapped hourly — [How do I get coins?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/how-do-i-get-coins-317)
- [official] Diamonds come from levels, quests, finished chapters, new Village Ranks and the wheel; some buildings give them hourly; bundles are sold in the shop — [How do I get diamonds?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/how-do-i-get-diamonds-318)
- [official] **Treasure Chest:** beating levels puts bonus diamonds in the chest. It can be opened from **200** diamonds and holds up to **400**. Past that, a win yields fewer diamonds. The opening cost is fixed regardless of the amount held. Harder levels award more — [Treasure Chest](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/treasure-chest-359)
- [official] Levels that award a building's objects are marked in the game — [How do I rebuild buildings?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/how-do-i-rebuild-buildings-305). Wooga's FAQ addresses players who finished all available levels three times, which supports the three-tier structure below — [Are more levels coming?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/are-more-levels-coming-319)
- [player] Each level has easy, medium and hard variants, differing in obstacles, goal counts or fewer moves. Hard can be replayed without limit for coins and diamonds, and triples or quintuples tournament points — [Mog Anarchy, Jan 2018](http://moganarchy.blogspot.com/2018/01/review-tropicats.html)
- [player] Coins and diamonds accrue across the 3 tiers with a larger bonus for higher tiers, but replaying the hardest tier stopped giving a bonus reward (Mar 2018). A 2019 reviewer asks what happened to the diamonds once given for passing levels. A 2019 reviewer near level 2000 says rewards do not change as the game goes on. Another puts daily-streak rewards at 2 to 7 diamonds (Sep 2019) — [App Store US reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850?see-all=reviews)
- [official] **Paws** are earned from Easy, Medium and Hard Challenges in a set of 3. **400 Paws** unlock one wheel spin for one of 4 decorations. Deco sets rotate every Sunday, and Paws carry over. Diamonds can swap out an unwanted Challenge — [What are the Challenges?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/what-are-the-challenges-387)
- Conflict: replay rewards. Mog Anarchy reports hard-tier replays still pay, while the March 2018 App Store review says the bonus is gone. Search also surfaced a Google Play review saying completed levels cannot be replayed for limited-time decorations; I did not read that review directly because the fetch was truncated.
- Conflict: "golden butterflies". A guide lists them alongside the currencies, earned from a 2×2 match ([Gamezebo, Nov 2017](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/tropicats-tips-cheats-and-strategies/)). Wooga describes the Butterfly as a board piece made by matching a square, which removes or weakens obstacles ([Butterfly](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/butterfly-335)). Treat it as a piece, not a currency.

### Inferences
- The tier system multiplies content: each level can be cleared three times, with rising pay. [estimate, from the official FAQ plus player descriptions]
- Reward rules look to have tightened over time, moving from direct diamonds toward the paid chest. This rests on player memory, so it is weak evidence.

### Gaps
- Coins and diamonds per win, per tier: not found.
- Any bonus for remaining moves: not found in any source. Do not assume one.
- The chest's opening price in diamonds or money is not stated. See the IAP section for a possible match.

## Lives, moves and boosters

### Takeaway
One life regenerates every 20 minutes [official]. A 5-move continue was 70 diamonds in 2020 [player]. The cap of 5 lives and every refill price are unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- [official] A life regenerates after **20 minutes**. Lives can also be requested from friends or refilled instantly with diamonds; no price is given — [How do I get more lives?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/how-do-i-get-more-lives-302)
- [player] A 2019 reviewer says accepting all inbox gifts gives only 5 lives, which hints at a cap of 5 but does not state it — [App Store US reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850?see-all=reviews)
- [official] Extra moves are sold for diamonds and add **5 moves**; no price is stated — [How do I get more moves?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/how-do-i-get-more-moves-303)
- [player] **5 moves = 70 diamonds; $2.99 = 100 diamonds; a cross hammer = 120 diamonds** (Jan 2020) — [App Store US reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850?see-all=reviews)
- [official] Wheel: watch an ad to spin for unlimited lives, diamonds or coins, with a **10-minute** wait between spins and a bonus gift every **5 spins** — [Wheel of Furtune](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/wheel-of-furtune-333)
- [player] Ads run 30 seconds; infinite lives last 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes. Comments on the post dispute the 10-minute timer, saying the wheel stops after a while, so a cap may exist — [Mog Anarchy, Jan 2018](http://moganarchy.blogspot.com/2018/01/review-tropicats.html). The Gamezebo guide reports about 10 to 15 minutes — [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/tropicats-tips-cheats-and-strategies/)
- [official] Level of the Day: 24 hours, 3 tries, a loss costs no life, chosen at random, and a win gives a special reward — [Level of the Day](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/level-of-the-day-358)
- [official] Daily Gifts: a daily reward, an extra gift for watching an ad, and better rewards every 5th day — [Daily Gifts](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/daily-gifts-354)
- [official] Booster list. Board pieces: Line Burster, Fruit Bomb, Butterfly, Color Paw, Fruit Stone. In-game: Hammer, Star Hammer, Color Hammer. Pre-game: Combo, Double Butterfly, Color Paw — [Elsie's Boosters](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/categories/elsies-boosters-2)
- [official] Effects read: the Hammer removes or weakens one fruit or obstacle ([Hammer](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/in-game-hammer-306)). The Star Hammer clears one row and one column, the same as matching two Line Bursters ([Star Hammer](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/in-game-star-hammer-310)). The pre-game Combo starts with one Line Burster and one Fruit Bomb ([Combo](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/pre-game-combo-323)). Mega Boost events upgrade the Hammer to 3×3 and Star Burst to 3 rows and 3 columns, on a timer ([Mega Boost](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/mega-boost-events-400))
- [third-party] A 4-in-a-row clears a row, a 5-in-a-row clears a colour, and powerups are unlocked periodically or given free — [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/tropicats-tips-cheats-and-strategies/)
- [player] Tournament top 10 win boosters; first place in January 2018 was 3 of each booster type plus a special hammer — [Mog Anarchy](http://moganarchy.blogspot.com/2018/01/review-tropicats.html)

### Inferences
- [estimate] At the 2020 reported rate of $2.99 per 100 diamonds ($0.03 each), a 5-move continue costs about $2.10 and a cross hammer about $3.60.
- [estimate] Connecting Facebook pays exactly one continue's worth of diamonds (70), if the 2020 price still applies. The 70 is [official]: [Why connect to Facebook?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/why-should-i-connect-to-facebook-322)
- [estimate] With a cap of 5 and 20-minute regeneration, a full refill takes 100 minutes. The cap is the unconfirmed part.

### Gaps
- Confirmed life cap, refill price, whether continue prices escalate within a level, booster prices beyond the one hammer figure, booster unlock levels, and the wheel's daily limit.
- Booster articles for Line Burster, Fruit Bomb, Color Paw, Fruit Stone, Color Hammer, Double Butterfly and pre-game Color Paw were listed but not read.

## How many levels per build, and what do builds cost?

### Takeaway
No source gives levels per build, individual prices or a cost curve. The best hard figure is 40 levels per chapter from one 2018 update. Story buildings cost **objects from marked levels**, not currency, while decorations cost coins or diamonds.

### Cited Findings
- [official] Each building needs its own objects, won from marked levels. When the rebuilding task in the Chief's Diary is done, the building appears — [How do I rebuild buildings?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/how-do-i-rebuild-buildings-305)
- [official] An August 2018 update added **2 chapters with 80 new levels, 240 challenges in total** when played from easy to difficult — [Wooga blog, 7 Aug 2018](https://www.wooga.com/blog/tropicats-behind-the-scenes-how-a-match-3-level-is-made-977a77ca9b4b)
- [player] One reviewer places level ~668 in episode 19 (Sep 2019) — [App Store US reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850?see-all=reviews)
- [player] At Chapter 28 in June 2022, a reviewer holds **170k coins and nearly 6k diamonds** with nothing to buy, because after an update most decorations and buildings are locked until **Chapter 64** — [App Store GB reviews](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850?see-all=reviews)
- [player] In January 2018 a reviewer complained about high prices on diamond-only decorations — [App Store GB reviews](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850?see-all=reviews)
- [player] One reviewer says a whole section can take days to a week late in the game (Jan 2020) — [App Store US reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850?see-all=reviews)

### Inferences
- [estimate] 80 levels × 3 tiers = 240, which fits the blog's wording. That gives **40 levels per chapter and 120 challenges per chapter** in that update. Wooga does not spell out this reading.
- [estimate] Level 668 in chapter 19 implies about 35 to 37 levels per chapter, close to 40. Level labels therefore seem to count levels rather than tiers.
- [estimate] A stockpile of 170k coins at Chapter 28 shows that income far outran purchasable items once content was chapter-gated. For our clone, the sink has to scale with chapter or the currency stops mattering.
- Escalation cannot be established. The only signal is the sink failure above, plus a reviewer saying rewards stay flat.

### Gaps
- Levels per quest or building, and objects per building.
- Prices of any decoration in coins or diamonds, Village points per item, and rank thresholds. The in-game rank panel shows these, so a short play session or screen recording would be the efficient way to get them.

## How does the meta loop work?

### Takeaway
Two parallel tracks. A **linear story quest list** (Chief's Diary) sends you to levels that award a building's objects. A **free decoration economy** spends coins and diamonds for Village points and rank. It is closer to a city decorator than to Homescapes' star-per-task list.

### Cited Findings
- [official] Destroyed huts and buildings become available as the story progresses. Once rebuilt, they yield coins or diamonds. Objects and decorations are bought in the shop for Village points and rank — [Let's rebuild the village!](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/lets-rebuild-the-village-304)
- [official] The notebook's Chief's Diary shows the current quest and can take the player to the next required level — [What to do next](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/i-dont-know-what-to-do-next-328)
- [official] Decorating earns Village points, which raise Village Rank and unlock rewards. The rank panel shows points needed and the next reward. Tiers are not numbered in the article — [Village Rank](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/whats-the-village-rank-321)
- [third-party] Decor can be repositioned freely during the refill wait, and area completion depends on quests and required components — [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/tropicats-tips-cheats-and-strategies/)
- [player] Both coins and diamonds buy decorations, which raise rank and cat happiness — [Mog Anarchy](http://moganarchy.blogspot.com/2018/01/review-tropicats.html)
- [official] If an item costs more coins than held, the difference can be bought with diamonds — [How do I get coins?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/how-do-i-get-coins-317)
- [official] Side metas:
  - **Ava's Workshop:** 4 deco items a week, built from blueprints in Mystery Boxes; unplaced progress resets weekly — [Ava's Workshop](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/what-is-avas-workshop-346)
  - **Seasonal Sets:** themed items for coins or diamonds, giving Village points and tokens toward a repeating Grand Prize — [Seasonal Sets](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/seasonal-sets-392)
  - **Collections:** a time-limited card album that furnishes the rooms of Elsie's house; duplicates convert to shells, and a full shell bar offers a choice of 3 rewards — [Collections](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/how-do-the-collections-work-394), [Shells](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/what-are-shells-397)
  - **Treasure Diving:** 8 special levels in a row without losing; a loss on 1–3 restarts from 1, a loss on 4–8 restarts from 4 — [Treasure Diving](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/treasure-diving-381)
  - **Pirate Breakout:** 10 timed levels with prizes at levels 3, 6 and 10 — [Pirate Breakout](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/pirate-breakout-382)
  - **Tournaments:** special fruit collection for points, unlocked at level 25 — [Tournament](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/whats-the-tournament-337)

### Inferences
Reconstructed loop [estimate on structure, each step sourced above]:
1. The Chief's Diary names a story task.
2. The player clears marked levels, which award that building's objects and pay coins and diamonds.
3. The task completes, the building appears, and it then yields coins or diamonds on an hourly tap.
4. The player spends coins and diamonds on placeable decorations, earning Village points.
5. Rank-ups pay coins and diamonds, which feed back into step 4.
6. Finished chapters pay diamonds and open more of the story.
7. Hard-tier replays and events provide extra income and exclusive decorations.

- Unlike Gardenscapes, story progress is not paid for with a per-level star, so decoration spending and story progress are largely decoupled. That matches the 2022 stockpile complaint.
- I saw no evidence of a choice between variants for a story building. Freedom appears to be in placing decorations.

### Gaps
- Whether decorations are ever required for story progress, whether buildings are placed by the player or in fixed spots, and the number of buildings per chapter.
- Tropicam and the Fruit Stone were listed but not read.

### Inferences

### Gaps

## Currencies and IAP price points

### Takeaway
Three spendable currencies matter: coins (soft), diamonds (hard), and event tokens such as Paws and shells. US prices run from $1.99 to $39.99 on the visible list.

### Cited Findings
- [official] US App Store IAPs shown (the page displays 10): Offer XS $1.99; Diamonds XS $2.99; Treasure Chest $3.99; Diamonds M $5.99; Daily Deal $9.99 (twice) and $19.99; Holiday Sale $9.99; Diamonds L $19.99; Diamonds XL $39.99 — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850)
- [player] $2.99 buys 100 diamonds (Jan 2020) — [App Store US reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850?see-all=reviews)
- [official] 70 free diamonds for connecting Facebook — [Why connect to Facebook?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/why-should-i-connect-to-facebook-322)
- [official] The listing notes loot boxes and ads and an 18+ rating — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tropicats-match-3-puzzle-game/id1215220850)
- [official] Ads reward players; Wooga says over 95% of players who want an ad can get one — [Ad availability](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/tropicats/articles/ad-availability-384)

### Inferences
- [estimate] The $3.99 Treasure Chest IAP is most likely the chest opening price, by name. That would make 200–400 diamonds cost about $0.01–0.02 each, against $0.03 for the XS pack. The chest would then be the best-value diamond purchase and a typical piggy bank.
- [estimate] Diamonds XS at $2.99 is probably the 100-diamond pack the reviewer describes.

### Gaps
- Diamond counts for M, L and XL, coin packs, bundle contents and whether prices have changed since 2020.
