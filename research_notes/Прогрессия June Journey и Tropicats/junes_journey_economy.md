# June's Journey (Wooga): progression economy with concrete numbers

Researched 2026-11-02. Labels: **[official]** Wooga help centre or App Store listing; **[wiki]** fan wiki on Fandom; **[player]** player Q&A; **[estimated]** my own arithmetic, with inputs shown.

How far to trust the sources:
- Help-centre pages and the App Store listing were read directly. Help-centre pages show no date, so treat them as live but undated.
- junes-journey.fandom.com returned HTTP 402 and appgamer.com returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches. **Every [wiki] and [player] number below comes from search-result excerpts, not a read of the full page.** Recheck any number that will drive tuning.
- Reddit is blocked to the search tool. Facebook groups and YouTube transcripts were not reached.
- Search excerpts did not always tie a claim to one page, so **individual AppGamer links are best matches from the result set**, and the exact answer may sit on a neighbouring thread.
- AppGamer answers are roughly 2020 to 2024 where a date is visible. No dated balance changelog was found.

Three assumptions in the brief do not match what I found:
1. **Stars and Flowers are thresholds, not currencies that get spent.** Stars never decrease, and Flowers count as a cumulative total.
2. **I found no XP and no key currency in the main loop.** Level is Flower level.
3. **I found no Time, Differences or Day/Night modes in regular scenes.** Those descriptions appear on G5 support pages for a different game, so do not copy them as June's Journey facts.

## What does one played scene pay, and how does pay vary?

### Takeaway
A play pays coins equal to floor(score / 10,000), plus 10 for a Perfect, so about 76 to 80 coins at most. It also adds star progress and sometimes a suitcase. The larger rewards come once per star, as Star Boxes. No source shows pay varying by mode, because regular scenes appear to have one mode.

### Cited Findings
**Coins per play**
- [wiki] Base coins = score / 10,000, rounded down; a Perfect adds 10 coins — [Hidden Object Scene](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Object_Scene)
- [official] Coins are paid every time a scene is played, and a PERFECT always gives a bonus; the amounts are not stated — [Wooga search result](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/how-do-i-play-junes-journey-394)

**Score**
- [wiki] Maximum 700,000 points per scene, split into base, accuracy, time and hints; base maximum 455,000 — [Hidden Object Scene](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Object_Scene)
- [player] Base maximum 454,000 (first 8 objects tapped in list order), accuracy 40,000, hint 40,000; top 5th-star score given as 662,000 or better. This conflicts slightly with the wiki — [AppGamer 49779](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/49779-how-do-you-earn-the-maximum-points), [AppGamer 41302](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/41302-how-does-scoring-work)
- [player] You cannot score as high while filling stars 1 to 4 as while filling the 5th — [AppGamer 41302](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/41302-how-does-scoring-work)
- [wiki] Objects to find: 8 at star 0, 10 at star 1, 12 at stars 2 to 5, with per-object points rising at higher stars — [Stars](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Stars)
- [official] Faster finds score higher, and wrong taps lose points. Hints are free and recharge over time — [What is a Hidden Object Scene?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-a-hidden-object-scene-439)

**Stars**
- [official] Up to 5 Stars per scene, each needing more points than the last; points from each play accumulate toward them — [How do I play](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/how-do-i-play-junes-journey-394)
- [third-party guide, player-grade] A run can earn a fraction of a star (examples 12% and 50%), and 3 to 4 replays may be needed for a full star — [BlueStacks DE, 2 May 2024](https://www.bluestacks.com/de/blog/game-guides/junes-journey/jj-tips-tricks-de.html)
- [player] The first scene of a chapter needs fewer points; the requirement rises with each star and each scene in the chapter — [AppGamer 14715](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/14715-how-do-you-get-the-stars-to-unlock)

**Suitcases (random bonus during a play)**
- [wiki] Coins 10 / 25 / 150 and Energy 1 / 5 / 15 for brown / red / purple. Compass only in purple. Gem Suitcase pays 5 diamonds, purple only. Drop rates not found — [Hidden Object Scene](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Object_Scene)
- [official] Bonus Energy and Compasses sometimes come from scenes — [Energy](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-do-i-need-energy-for-and-how-do-i-get-it-492), [Compasses](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-are-the-compasses-for-and-how-do-i-find-them-332)

**Modes and mastery**
- [official] Regular scenes use a list of items at the bottom of the screen. The help pages name no other regular-scene mode — [What is a Hidden Object Scene?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-a-hidden-object-scene-439)
- [official] Word Mode and Silhouette Mode exist in the Secrets event, which has its own Secret Energy and diamond-priced hints — [How do I play Secrets?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/how-do-i-play-secrets-196)

- [wiki] The scene is shown darkened before play; this is a preview, not a mode — [Hidden Object Scene](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Object_Scene)
- [player] A mastered scene counts 5 stars, and beating your own score pays little or nothing beyond the normal reward — [AppGamer 46800](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/46800-is-it-better-to-master-every-scene), [AppGamer 38690](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/38690-how-do-you-beat-your-own-score-to-a)

### Inferences
- [estimated] Coin ceiling per play: floor(662,000 / 10,000) + 10 = 76, or 80 at the wiki's 700,000. Early-star plays must pay less, because fewer objects and lower per-object points mean lower scores.
- [estimated] Implied speed component: 662,000 − 454,000 − 40,000 − 40,000 = 128,000 points at the player-reported top score.
- Pay varies by score, which moves with star tier. No source shows a coin multiplier for star tier, mode or mastery beyond that.

### Gaps
- Typical real scores by star tier, and so typical coins per play. Only the formula and the ceiling are sourced.
- Suitcase drop rates and material contents.
- Whether Adventure Scenes pay coins or cost energy.
- Whether the Stars-page object table and score maximum are still current.

## What does energy cost, and how are refills priced?

### Takeaway
A scene costs 15 energy (10 with an ad discount). Energy regenerates 1 per 2 minutes up to a 110 cap, or 150 with a one-time purchase. A full bar costs 59 diamonds according to the wiki. Free regen allows about 48 plays a day.

### Cited Findings
- [official] 15 energy per scene; 1 energy per 2 minutes until full; bar 110; extended bar 150 at the same rate; extended bar includes a one-time 3x refill worth 450 energy; if already owned, the 3x refill appears in the Shop instead — [Energy](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-do-i-need-energy-for-and-how-do-i-get-it-492), [Extended Energy Bar](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-the-extended-energy-bar-334)
- [official] Scenes can cost 10 instead of 15 while an energy discount is active — [Help Wanted](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/help-wanted-cases-393)
- [wiki] Watching an ad gives the discount for 30 minutes, repeatable many times a day, and it does not apply to Club Competitions — [Energy](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Energy). A January 2023 guide says about a 30-second ad with no daily limit — [gamepedia.jp](https://gamepedia.jp/archives/85755)
- [wiki] One full bar costs 59 diamonds. The permanent extension costs 799 diamonds and includes "an additional 150" energy, which conflicts with the official 450 — [Energy](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Energy)
- [player] 799 diamonds confirmed by buyers; after purchase the offer becomes 3 bars for 139 diamonds, while another buyer reports 159. Date of change unknown — [AppGamer 44763](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/44763-theres-an-offer-to-upgrade-energy-f), [AppGamer 13424](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/13424-i-just-spent-799-diamonds-for-what)
- [player] Players say the bar fills in 3h20 at 110 and 5h at 150 — [AppGamer 13424](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/13424-i-just-spent-799-diamonds-for-what)
- [wiki] A Star Booster gives one full bar — [Energy](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Energy). [player] 150 energy per completed character puzzle and per booster with the extended bar — [AppGamer 13424](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/13424-i-just-spent-799-diamonds-for-what)
- [official] Energy also comes from Star Boxes (table above) — [Probabilities](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-the-average-probability-for-star-boxes-and-memoirs-pack-rewards-390)

### Inferences
- [estimated] Free regen is 30 energy/hour, or 720/day. That is 2 plays/hour and 48 plays/day at 15, or 3 plays/hour and 72 plays/day at 10.
- [estimated] A full 110 bar is 7 plays (11 with discount), and refilling it from empty takes 3h40. The players' 3h20 does not match the official rate (3h20 is only 100 energy). 150 at 2 minutes is 5h, which does match.
- [estimated] At 59 diamonds per 110 energy, a play costs about 8.0 diamonds, or about 5.4 with the discount.

### Gaps
- A current official refill price list: the help pages give none, so 59 and 139/159 are unconfirmed for 2026.
- Whether reward energy can stack above the cap. Players ask about holding thousands, but I did not confirm the mechanics.

## How are chapters structured, how many exist, and what is the cadence?

### Takeaway
Each chapter is 5 Hidden Object Scenes plus 1 Adventure Scene, and one chapter ships weekly. The count today is uncertain: one source says about 355 as of July 2026, but extrapolating from dated data suggests about 450 to 490.

### Cited Findings
- [official] 5 Hidden Object Scenes and 1 Adventure Scene per chapter — [How do I play](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/how-do-i-play-junes-journey-394)
- [official] App Store: "New Chapters Weekly"; version 4.16.3 dated Oct 26 (year not shown); 4.6 from 328K ratings — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/junes-journey-hidden-objects/id1200391796)
- [player] Volume 1 has 100 chapters (scenes 1 to 500), Volume 2 has 50 (501 to 750), Volume 3 has 25 (751 to 875), Volumes 4 and 5 have 35 each — [AppGamer 35613](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/35613-what-are-the-3-volumes), [AppGamer 54792](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/54792-how-many-rooms-are-in-volume-5)
- [player] One chapter every Thursday, with exceptions for holidays and between volumes; Volume 6 chapter 40 (scenes 1421 to 1425) was newest in December 2022; 295 playable in 2023 (February per one answer) — [AppGamer 36737](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/36737-how-many-chapters-are-there-in-tota)
- [player] After a one-week break, Volume 8's first chapter was due 29 February 2024 — [AppGamer 85932](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/85932-when-will-volume-8-be-released)
- [third-party guide] "350+" chapters, table numbered up to 355, updated 28 July 2026 — [mobi.gg](https://mobi.gg/en/tips/how-many-chapters-junes-journey/)
- [player] Islands named by players include Sycamore, Sunflower and Solomon's Shelf, alongside the official Orchid Island — [AppGamer 94722](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/94722-does-anyone-else-have-to-earn-360-s), [AppGamer 36040](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/36040-just-got-to-sunflower-island-and-36), [AppGamer 94647](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/94647-i-am-on-solomons-shelf-island-and-i)

### Inferences
- [estimated] The volume sizes check out: 100 + 50 + 25 + 35 + 35 + 40 = 285 chapters, and 285 × 5 = scene 1425, matching the December 2022 data point.
- [estimated] From chapter 285 in December 2022, about 205 weeks to November 2026 gives up to about 490 chapters, or about 450 to 490 after breaks. Mobi.gg's 355 would imply only about 70 chapters in nearly four years, so it is probably a stale table despite its 2026 date. Treat the total as unresolved.

### Gaps

- Mapping of volumes to islands.

## What does it cost to unlock a scene or chapter, and how many plays does that imply?

### Takeaway
Nothing is spent on an unlock. Each gate is a threshold: both clues from the previous scene (3 stars plus a further play), a Flower level, and 20 of 25 chapter stars for the Adventure Scene. That implies roughly 60 to 85 plays per chapter, which is an estimate. Flower requirements run about 1,300 to 1,970 per chapter.

### Cited Findings
**Star and clue gates**
- [official] First clue on the first play; second clue on a play after earning the 3rd Star; new scenes need both clues plus enough Flowers — [What is a Hidden Object Scene?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-a-hidden-object-scene-439)
- [official] Example: Scene 3 needs Flower Level 3 and both clues in Scene 2 — [Why is a scene locked?](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/why-is-a-hidden-object-scene-locked-315)
- [official] Adventure Scene needs at least 20 Stars in that chapter; next chapter needs the Adventure Scene done plus a level requirement — [Levels](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-are-june-s-journey-levels-396), [Next chapter](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/how-do-i-unlock-the-next-chapter-316)
- [wiki] Stars page also says 20 stars and all 20 clues are needed to finish a chapter. That clue count does not obviously fit 5 scenes at 2 clues each, so it may be out of date or may include something else — [Stars](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Stars)

**Flower gates (cumulative; surplus carries over)**
- [player] Volume 1 chapters start at 170 flowers and rise to 1,760 (average 1,420); Volumes 2 to 4 average about 1,660; Volumes 6 to 8 average 1,968 — [AppGamer 57094](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/57094-how-many-flowers-do-you-need-for-ea)
- [player] Other estimates: 1,300 to 1,750, 1,500 to 1,760, and 1,200 to 1,900 per chapter — [AppGamer 36321](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/36321-how-many-flowers-are-needed-to-move), [AppGamer 32703](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/32703-how-many-flowers-does-it-take-to-ge)
- [player] Per level 70 to 530 depending on which scene it opens. The 2nd scene is cheapest (60 to 100, usually 70 to 90); the 3rd is 200 to 275; opening a chapter's first scene costs about the same as the 5th — [AppGamer 28581](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/28581-how-many-flowers-do-i-need-to-go-up), [AppGamer 57094](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/57094-how-many-flowers-do-you-need-for-ea)
- [player] Extra flowers lower the next level's requirement — [AppGamer 32331](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/32331-if-you-have-more-flowers-than-you-n)

### Inferences
All [estimated], using 3 to 4 plays per star from the 2024 BlueStacks guide. Early scenes are probably cheaper, so these are likely high for the first chapters.

| Milestone | Plays | Energy at 15 | Hours of free regen | Hours with ad discount |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Next scene (3 stars + 1 clue play) | 10 to 13 | 150 to 195 | 5 to 6.5 | 3.3 to 4.3 |
| Adventure gate (20 stars) | 60 to 85 | 900 to 1,275 | 30 to 43 | 20 to 28 |
| Full mastery (25 stars) | 75 to 100 | 1,125 to 1,500 | 38 to 50 | 25 to 33 |

- Against one chapter a week, energy alone lets a caught-up player finish a chapter in about two days, so the content cadence is the wall at the top. For new players the likely wall is Flowers (next section).
- Official wording implies about one Flower level per scene (Scene 3, Level 3), so about 5 levels per chapter. This is a single example, not a stated rule.

### Gaps
- A per-chapter Flower table for chapters 1 to 50. Players say private lists exist, but none was retrievable.
- Exact point thresholds per star. The wiki excerpt shows object counts and a maximum but no cutoff table, and no source gives measured plays per star by chapter.

## What do plots, buildings and decorations cost, and are they required?

### Takeaway
Decorating is required indirectly, because Flowers gate every scene and chapter. Coin decorations convert at exactly 30 coins per flower in sampled items, which makes a chapter's flowers worth about 43,000 to 59,000 coins. Plots are gated by total stars plus compasses, buildings by materials and timers.

### Cited Findings
**Flowers from coins**
- [official] Flowers come from placing decorations, upgrading buildings, restoring landmarks and Jack's Deliveries; selling removes them permanently, and storing removes them until replaced — [Levels](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-are-june-s-journey-levels-396)
- [wiki] Holly Bush 450 coins, 3h build, 15 flowers — [Forest Festivities Set](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Forest_Festivities_Set)
- [wiki] Wedding Cake 3,000 coins, about 21h40 build, 100 flowers — [Wonderful Wedding Set](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Wonderful_Wedding_Set)
- [wiki] A Grand Entrance 27,000 coins, 3d12h build, 900 flowers — [Blooming Botanicals Set](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Blooming_Botanicals_Set)
- [player] 30 coins per flower in the permanent and seasonal stores; about 2.6 diamonds per flower for diamond items; Limited Edition items cost considerably more — [AppGamer 53370](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/53370-im-trying-to-work-out-the-best-stra)
- [player] A decoration's flowers count only after construction finishes and the ribbon is removed — [AppGamer 32013](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/32013-im-confused-about-the-flowers-if-it)

**Buildings and passive income**
- [official] Buildings need materials and time; most can be upgraded for more Flowers; some generate Coins; landmarks take a long time and matter for progression — [How do I play](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/how-do-i-play-junes-journey-394)
- [wiki] Estate: 1 coin per 10 minutes, cap 60; copper material; 165 flowers at level 5; golden facade 400 gold bars raises it to 215. Other buildings: 1 coin per 40 minutes, cap 15 — [Estate](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Estate), [Buildings](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Buildings)
- [official] Jack's Deliveries unlock after Chapter 7, take materials (paint, wood, bricks) and gold, offer 2 or 3 destinations, and let diamonds cover missing materials (testing, not for all players). Wooga recommends upgrading buildings first — [Jack's Deliveries](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-jack-s-deliveries-422)

**Plots**
- [official] Compasses open new land regions, are "quite rare", and come from Star Boxes, scene bonuses, and sales for diamonds or money; no per-region amounts — [Compasses](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-are-the-compasses-for-and-how-do-i-find-them-332)
- [player] Each plot has a total-star and compass requirement; star boxes are not needed. Sunflower Island plot 1: 2,090 stars and 20 compasses; plot 2: 2,121 stars and 30 compasses (post about 2,200 days old, so around 2020) — [AppGamer 36040](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/36040-just-got-to-sunflower-island-and-36)
- [player] Sycamore Island: first 3 plots need 1 more star, the 4th needs 53 more, the rest 60 more each — [AppGamer 94722](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/94722-does-anyone-else-have-to-earn-360-s)
- [wiki] Tip Shop sells a compass for 800 tips — [Tip Shop](https://junes-journey.fandom.com/wiki/Tip_Shop)

### Inferences
- [estimated] All three wiki samples divide to exactly 30 coins per flower, confirming the player figure. A linear coins-to-flowers rate looks like the design rule.
- [estimated] Chapter flowers in coins at 30 each: 1,420 flowers is 42,600; 1,760 is 52,800; 1,968 is 59,040.
- [estimated] Coins from play per chapter: 60 to 85 plays × an assumed 50 to 76 coins is 3,000 to 6,460. That is only about 5 to 15% of the coin price of a chapter's flowers. The 50-coin floor is my assumption, not sourced. The gap must be filled by building upgrades, Star Box coins, events and rewards, or by replaying beyond the chapter gate. This is the most valuable number to verify in a real play session.
- [estimated] Passive coins: Estate at most 144/day if collected every 10h; each other coin building at most 36/day.

### Gaps
- Material costs and timers per building level, flowers per building upgrade, and landmark costs.
- Share of flowers from coins versus buildings versus events for a typical player.
- Compass amounts for Orchid Island's early plots.

## What happens in the first 30 minutes and first few chapters?

### Takeaway
I found no measured account of the first 30 minutes. Only the gates and a few early numbers are sourced, so copying this part accurately requires an instrumented play session on a fresh install.

### Cited Findings
- [official] Chapter gates: Travels from Chapter 3 Scene 2; Jack's Deliveries after Chapter 7; Café and Memoirs Help Wanted cases need those features unlocked first — [Travels](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-travels-449), [Jack's Deliveries](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/what-is-jack-s-deliveries-422), [Help Wanted](https://wooga.theymes.com/hc/en/junes-journey/articles/help-wanted-cases-393)
- [player] Volume 1 flower requirements begin at 170 per chapter, against a 1,420 average — [AppGamer 57094](https://www.appgamer.com/junes-journey-hidden-object/answers/57094-how-many-flowers-do-you-need-for-ea)
- [third-party guide, January 2023] Login bonus with a decoration at 30 consecutive days; one free bush per day and up to 8 coin bushes per day; stamp card gives energy at 5 and 10 points; the guide advises against rerolling since energy regen makes early start time matter more — [gamepedia.jp](https://gamepedia.jp/archives/85755)
- [third-party guide] Chapter 1 is "Arrival in New York", with the Gatehouse as its Adventure Scene — [mobi.gg](https://mobi.gg/en/tips/how-many-chapters-junes-journey/)

### Inferences
- [estimated] A full starting bar, if the game starts full, is 7 plays. With 8 to 10 object scenes and fractional stars, that is the natural end of the first session unless tutorial rewards top it up. This is a conditional estimate, not an observation.
- [estimated] The 170 versus 1,420 flower difference means the early chapter gate is about 8 times lighter than the Volume 1 average, so early flower generosity is real. Where the ramp ends is unknown.
