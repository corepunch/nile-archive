Use case: precise-object-edit
Create ONE transparent six-sprite game atlas in a 3-column by 2-row grid. The first reference is the existing chain atlas and the style/edit target. Retain its chunky alternating polished oval chain links and metal colors. The second reference is the exact flat-top hex cell silhouette: it has horizontal top and bottom edges, four 60-degree diagonal edges, and its left/right points are halfway up. It is a geometry guide only. Do not draw the tile itself.
Each sprite is a foreground restraint stretched across an imaginary hex tile, with both ends visibly fastened to the sloped tile edges by flat metal attachment plates. The plates are just inside the outline, oriented parallel to the edge and physically touch it with the outside side of the plate. Each compact plate has a little bolted foot and a dark recessed eye which visibly accepts the end link. The hex must be absent from the actual output and holes between links stay transparent. Design the hardware and links to read at 48 pixels.
Column 1 is one polished silver strand anchored to the LOWER LEFT and UPPER RIGHT diagonal edges; diagonal across center with a gentle slack bend, with fairly thin links to keep the puzzle piece readable. Column 2 is an independently painted golden single strand with the same weight and hardware at the UPPER LEFT and LOWER RIGHT diagonal edges. Column 3 is two distinct slim silver strands forming a clear X, one diagonal in each direction with a clean single overlap, fastened by FOUR separately visible plates at exactly those same four diagonal edge locations. The middle of each chain stays near the center of the imaginary tile. Leave plenty of open negative space above and below the chain; a restrained sag and no floating endpoints.
Top row is Relic Rush with warm burnished silver and muted gold. Bottom row is Christmas Market with cool bright silver and rich gold. Same state order and identical placement in both rows. Keep the six sprites separate with generous transparent gutters. View directly facing the flat tile, no visible tile, stones, gem, chip, padlock, outline, guide, numbering, words or backdrop. Only chains and their metal attachment plates on clean true alpha.
