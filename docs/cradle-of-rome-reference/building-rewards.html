Campaign building rewards

Every row is one purchase. Its reward is the piece that appears on later boards. A resource piece earns the amount shown. Matching 30 tool pieces charges the tool once before optional upgrades.

The order, resource values and price currencies match the Windows reference. The games use their own prices, themes and four permanent tool slots. Open the standalone Cradle of Rome 2 export.
