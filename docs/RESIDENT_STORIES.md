# Resident story and interaction plan

The player changes these stories by building a home, listening at it, and taking a detail to the other person. Each pair has a finished piece of work that the player can ask about from either side.

| Story beat | Relic Rush | Christmas Market |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move in | Nera opens the Cartographer’s House after expedition 56. Lio comes to the Great Aviary after 81. | Otis opens the Station Kiosk after wish 46. Ada comes to the Glassblower’s Stall after 71. |
| Get to know them | Nera shares her father’s way of checking a bearing. Lio admits his bird copies his snore. Those conversations can change levels 67 and 91. | Otis teaches Pip on his father’s tin lid. Ada plays the spoons. Those conversations can change wishes 63 and 90. |
| Meet | Lio asks Nera to draw his bird for his sister. Hearing about Mara’s sketch changes how he gets the bird to pose. | Ada makes Otis a blue draughts button. Asking Otis about the dent changes what he tells her about the board. |
| Hear each side | His sister recognizes Lio’s bitten ear. Nera explains why she kept drawing it. Take either detail to the other home. | Ada wins with a double jump. Otis taught her the move. Take either account to the other home. |
| Meet again at a level | On flood watch at 92, Lio offers to draw Nera. | After Pip’s telegram at 85, Ada and Otis give him a game while he waits. |
| Find out how it ended | His sister hangs the portraits facing each other. Nera is hunting for the pencil behind her ear. | Pip wins Ada’s blue button with a trap. Otis lets him play it unaided. |

## Visit flow

- Require the invitation and the building before introducing the resident. Require both introductions before their meeting.
- Offer personal questions and questions about the latest witnessed milestone. Keep the shared-story questions available across later milestones. At the second level scene, distinguish neither account, each resident alone, and both.
- Light the roof bubble whenever a question is available. The building and bubble open the same visit. Start a sole question immediately. For several, show the resident’s name, the questions and a way back to town.
- After an answer, offer the remaining questions and an exit. The last answer returns the player to town. Each final visit gives its own ending; hearing the other side first changes both the question and the reply.
- Save the chosen question immediately. Resume an interrupted answer and credit its memory when it finishes or is skipped. If the level scene and meeting happen in the opposite order, use the short town exchange to bring the pair up to date, including on older saves.

## Acceptance

Play both 100-level campaigns. Check both visiting orders, an interrupted answer, reopening the game, declining a question, and visiting after the finale. Read the densest question menu and the longest labels at 480 × 900 and 390 × 844. Check the roof bubble, a direct question, the continuation menu and the final return to town in the running games.
