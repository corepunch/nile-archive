# Privacy policy for Relic Rush and Christmas at Bellmere

Effective November 22, 2026

Igor Chernakov publishes Relic Rush – Uncover Mysteries and Christmas at
Bellmere. This policy applies to both games on iPhone.

## On your device

The games save your puzzle progress, town layout, and settings on your device.
They have no developer account, advertising, tracking, or external analytics.
The publisher does not receive a copy of your campaign save. Game progress is
kept until you remove the game and its data from your device. Apple handles any
device backups you enable.

## Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the games send your leaderboard scores
and achievements to Apple. They display the scores, nicknames, and avatars that
Game Center returns. The publisher does not store a separate copy of this data
on its servers or use it for marketing. [Apple explains how Game Center handles
your data and which activity other players can see.](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center/)

You can play the campaign without Game Center. Sign out of Game Center in
iPhone Settings to stop future score and achievement submissions. To manage
or request deletion of data held by Apple, use [Apple Data and Privacy](https://privacy.apple.com/).

## Support

For privacy questions or requests, email [Igor Chernakov](mailto:igor.chernakov@gmail.com).
If you contact support, your address and message are used to answer your request.
